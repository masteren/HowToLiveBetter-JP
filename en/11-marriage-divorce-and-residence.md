# 11. Marriage, divorce and residence: procedures and red lines for international marriages

<!-- zh-intro: 255d4b80 -->

This section covers the procedures for marrying, separating and divorcing in Japan, and how each affects your residence. The provisions of the Act on General Rules for Application of Laws, the Penal Code and the Immigration Act have been checked on e-Gov, and the Immigration Services Agency's materials are as of 2026-10-03. Whether your country of origin requires a separate registration, or recognizes the Japanese procedures, differs by country; follow your own embassy's or consulate's guidance.

### 1. Do not enter a "sham marriage" for a visa: registering a false marriage in the family register is a crime, and your status of residence will be revoked
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 不做「假结婚」换签证：户籍上登了假婚姻要判刑，在留资格会被取消 | 228d449f -->
- Cost: Free. If someone offers to pay you for a "paper marriage" to get them a visa, just say no.
- In plain words: Filing a marriage notice without a real marriage, just to get a visa, is a crime. Making a false report to an official so that untrue content is entered in the family register carries up to 5 years. Residence permission obtained by such deception will be revoked, and the person will also be punished. Japanese nationals or foreigners who lend their names to such marriages are in the same trouble.
- Benefit: The Penal Code provides that making a false report to a public official so that a false entry is made in the original of a notarized deed concerning rights and duties, such as a family register, is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years or a fine of up to 500,000 yen, and attempts are also punished. The Immigration Act provides that obtaining landing permission or residence permission by deception or other wrongful means is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 3 million yen, or both; where permission was obtained by deception or other wrongful means, the Minister of Justice may revoke the status of residence, and this is a category for which no departure preparation period is given
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 刑法 第百五十七条第一項・第三項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/140AC0000000045> ; 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第七十条第一項第二号の二、第二十二条の四第一項第一号・第二号・第七項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: Do not believe brokers who say "it's just a formality" or "everyone does it".

### 2. Separating from your spouse, being in divorce mediation, or escaping domestic violence does not count as "6 months of not living as a spouse", so your status will not be revoked for it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 和配偶分居、正在离婚调停或者躲避家暴，不算「6 个月不以配偶身份生活」，在留不会因此被取消 | 77778f46 -->
- Cost: Free. Keep documents that show your situation: police reports of domestic violence, medical certificates, acceptance papers for divorce mediation. If you are suffering domestic violence, first call 110, or call #8008 to be connected to a nearby spousal violence counselling and support center.
- In plain words: People on a spouse visa who do not live as a spouse for 6 months in a row can have their status revoked. But immigration has stated several cases in which it does not revoke. Escaping domestic violence is not revoked. Living apart for unavoidable reasons such as raising children, while still sharing a livelihood, is not revoked. Going back to your country of origin for a long time because a relative is seriously ill, and being in divorce mediation or litigation, are also not revoked. Do not put up with domestic violence for fear of losing your visa.
- Benefit: The "concrete examples in which status of residence is not revoked", published in 2012 by the Ministry of Justice's Immigration Bureau (now the Immigration Services Agency), list: 1. needing temporary refuge or protection because of the spouse's violence (DV); 2. living apart from the spouse for unavoidable reasons such as raising children, while sharing the same livelihood; 3. leaving Japan for a long period with a re-entry permit (including the special re-entry permit) for reasons such as injury or illness of relatives in the home country; 4. being in divorce mediation or a divorce lawsuit. Whether to revoke is judged case by case, and is not limited to these examples. Even after 6 months or more of not living as a spouse, a change to another status of residence may be permitted where, for example, the person is supporting and raising their own child of Japanese nationality. The Immigration Act provides that when revoking on this ground, consideration must be given to allowing the person to apply for a change of status or for permanent residence. The Cabinet Office states that calling the nationwide number #8008 connects you to the nearest prefectural spousal violence counselling and support center, at normal call charges
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 法務省入国管理局 (2012). 配偶者の身分を有する者としての活動を行わないことに正当な理由がある場合等在留資格の取消しを行わない具体例について. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/920000161.pdf> ; 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十二条の四第一項第七号、第二十二条の五. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 内閣府男女共同参画局「DV相談について」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.gender.go.jp/policy/no_violence/dv_navi/index.html>
- Notes: The Immigration Services Agency has Simplified and Traditional Chinese versions on the same page. After a divorce or your spouse's death, you must report to immigration within 14 days; see Section 2, entry 3 (reporting after leaving a job, changing jobs or changing schools). The Cabinet Office also runs "DV Soudan Plus" (DV相談＋), with 24-hour phone and online chat consultation.

### 3. Around a divorce, do not take your child to your country of origin without telling the other parent: if both countries are parties to the Hague Convention, a court may order the child returned to the country where they lived
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 离婚前后别不打招呼带孩子回原籍国：两国都加入了海牙公约的，法院可能命令把孩子送回原来住的国家 | 652f5dc6 -->
- Cost: Free. If you want to take your child to your country of origin for a long time, first get the other parent's consent, ideally in writing.
- In plain words: Suppose the child is under 16, and both the country where they lived and the country they are now in are parties to the Hague Convention. If one parent takes the child abroad without the other's consent, the other parent can apply to have the child returned. Japanese courts apply this rule to children brought into Japan. If the other parent agreed beforehand or accepted it afterwards, the court may decline to order the return.
- Benefit: Article 27 of the Act for Implementation of the Hague Convention, under which Japan implements the Convention, provides that the court must order the child's return when all of the following are met: the child is under 16; the child is in Japan; the removal or retention breaches the applicant's rights of custody under the law of the State of habitual residence; and at the time of removal the State of habitual residence was a Contracting State. Article 28 of that Act lists grounds for refusing return: the application was made more than a year after removal and the child has settled in the new environment; the applicant was not actually exercising custody rights at the time; the applicant consented beforehand or acquiesced afterwards; there is a grave risk that return would expose the child to physical or psychological harm or an intolerable situation; the child objects to return and has reached an age at which their views should be taken into account; and so on. In judging "grave risk", whether the child would be subject to violence from the applicant is considered
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国際的な子の奪取の民事上の側面に関する条約の実施に関する法律 第 2 条、第 27 条、第 28 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/425AC0000000048>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. Whether your country or region of origin is a Contracting State, and from what date it is in force with Japan, must be checked on the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' list of Contracting States; this book has not checked each one. This rule concerns whether the child must be returned in civil law; the parent who took the child may also face criminal liability in other countries, depending on that country's law. If you are escaping domestic violence, go to the police first; violence is expressly among the grounds for refusing return. For how your residence is treated, see entry 2 of this section (escaping domestic violence). The benefit is rated large because a court may order the child returned.

### 4. After April 2026, if you divorce by agreement without settling child support, the parent raising the child can still claim statutory child support of 20,000 yen per child per month
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 2026 年 4 月以后协议离婚没谈养育费，带孩子的一方也能按月要每个孩子 2 万日元的法定养育费 | 6267b3d6 -->
- Cost: Asking costs nothing. If the other parent does not pay, you have to apply to the family court for mediation or a ruling, or go to compulsory enforcement, which takes time.
- In plain words: For divorces by agreement on or after April 1, 2026 without an agreement on child support, the parent mainly raising the child can claim statutory child support (法定養育費) from the other. It is 20,000 yen per child per month, counted from the day of the divorce. This is only a temporary floor. The real amount still has to be agreed between the two or set by a court. If the other parent can show they cannot afford it, they may pay less or nothing.
- Benefit: Article 766-3 of the Civil Code (in force from April 1, 2026) provides that where parents divorce by agreement without agreeing on the cost of the child's care, the parent mainly caring for the child after the divorce may claim from the other, at the end of each month, an amount calculated under Ministry of Justice ordinance, from the date of divorce until the earliest of the date child support is agreed, the date a ruling on child support becomes final, or the date the child reaches adulthood; the other parent may refuse all or part of it by proving they cannot pay, or that paying would make their life markedly hard. The amount set by the Ministry of Justice ordinance is 20,000 yen a month times the number of children: 40,000 yen a month for two children, 60,000 yen a month for three
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 民法 第 766 条の 3. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/129AC0000000089>；法務省「養育費に関する法務省令の概要」. <https://www.moj.go.jp/content/001452582.pdf>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. The 20,000 yen is a floor when nothing was agreed, not a standard amount; the real amount, based on both parents' incomes, usually has to be negotiated or set by a court. Japanese law applies under the same conditions as in entry 6 of this section (both parents as holders of parental authority). The benefit is rated medium at over 200,000 yen a year, more with more children.

### 5. To marry in Japan, file a marriage notice at the ward office; the foreign partner attaches a "certificate of legal capacity to marry" from their embassy or consulate, with a Japanese translation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 在日本结婚，去区役所交婚姻届，外国人要附本国使领馆开的「婚姻要件具备证明书」和日文译文 | 76d1f275 -->
- Cost: Getting the certificate from your embassy or consulate costs a fee and one or two trips. All documents in other languages need Japanese translations.
- In plain words: A foreigner marrying a Japanese national in Japan, or two foreigners marrying in Japan, can file a marriage notice (婚姻届) at the ward office, and the marriage is formed once it is accepted. The foreigner submits a certificate of legal capacity to marry (婚姻要件具備証明書) from their embassy or consulate, showing they can marry under their own country's law, for example that they are of legal age and currently single. If your country does not issue this certificate, there are alternatives; ask at the counter.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Justice states that when a Japanese national and a foreigner, or two foreigners, marry in Japan, they submit a marriage notice at the family register counter, and once it is confirmed that both meet the requirements for marriage and the notice is accepted, the marriage validly takes effect; notices between two foreigners are kept for 50 years. If foreigners have registered their marriage at their country's embassy or consulate in Japan in their country's form, they do not need to file again at a Japanese counter. When a foreigner files a marriage notice, a certificate of legal capacity to marry and its Japanese translation are required; this is a document in which the ambassador, minister or consul of their country certifies that the person meets the requirements for marriage under their country's law; some countries do not issue it, and there are other methods. The Act on General Rules for Application of Laws provides that the requirements for forming a marriage follow each party's national law; the form of marriage follows the law of the place of celebration, or may follow one party's national law, but when celebrated in Japan with one party a Japanese national, it must follow the Japanese form
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 法務省「国際結婚、海外での出生等に関する戸籍Ｑ＆Ａ」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/MINJI/minji15.html> ; 法の適用に関する通則法 第二十四条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/418AC0000000078>
- Notes: The benefit is rated medium because whether the marriage is valid directly decides whether you can apply for a spouse visa. After registering in Japan, your country of origin may also require a separate report or registration; Chinese nationals should ask a Chinese embassy or consulate in Japan. If you need to switch to a spouse visa after marrying, apply separately to immigration for a change of status; for the documents required at renewal, follow the Immigration Services Agency's pages.

### 6. From April 2026, divorcing parents can agree to both hold parental authority, and for children of Japanese nationality Japanese law applies; where there is domestic violence or abuse, the court names only one parent
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 2026 年 4 月起协议离婚可以选父母双方都当亲权人，孩子是日本籍的照日本法来；有家暴或虐待的，法院只定一方 | 92ff4653 -->
- Cost: Free. Before divorcing, the two of you need to agree on who holds parental authority; if you cannot agree, apply to the family court for mediation or a ruling.
- In plain words: From April 1, 2026, a divorce by agreement in Japan can name both parents as holders of parental authority (親権者), or only one. If they cannot agree, a court decides. Where there is a risk of child abuse or domestic violence, the court must name only one. If one spouse is a Japanese national living in Japan, Japanese law applies to the divorce. If the child has the same nationality as one parent, the parent-child relationship follows the child's national law.
- Benefit: Article 819 of the Civil Code (in force from April 1, 2026) provides that in a divorce by agreement the parents decide by agreement whether both or one will hold parental authority; in a judicial divorce the court decides on both or one; if agreement fails, the family court may rule on the request of the father or mother. In deciding, the court must name only one where the father or mother may harm the child physically or mentally, or where one parent may suffer violence or the like from the other and joint exercise of parental authority would be difficult. Article 27 of the Act on General Rules for Application of Laws provides that when one spouse is a Japanese national habitually resident in Japan, Japanese law applies to the divorce. Article 32 of that Act provides that when the child's national law is the same as the father's or mother's, the legal relationship between parent and child follows the child's national law, and otherwise the law of the child's habitual residence
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 民法 第 819 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/129AC0000000089>；法の適用に関する通則法 第 27 条、第 32 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/418AC0000000078>；法務省「民法等の一部を改正する法律（父母の離婚後等の子の養育に関する見直し）について〔令和８年４月１日施行〕」. <https://www.moj.go.jp/MINJI/minji07_00357.html>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. When two foreigners divorce in Japan, the law applied is often their common national law; see entry 7 of this section (two foreigners divorcing in Japan). Whether joint parental authority is recognized in your country of origin must be asked at its embassy or consulate, and rules in other countries and regions may differ. The benefit is rated medium because it affects who makes decisions for the child in future. For how your residence is treated while escaping domestic violence, see entry 2 of this section (escaping domestic violence does not count as "6 months of not living as a spouse").

### 7. When two foreigners divorce in Japan, the law applied is often their common national law; after filing a divorce notice at the ward office, first ask your embassy or consulate whether your country recognizes it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 两个外国人在日本离婚，适用的往往是两人共同的本国法；在区役所交了离婚届，原籍国认不认要先问使领馆 | d5d5f097 -->
- Cost: Free. Before divorcing, ask your embassy or consulate whether a divorce done in Japan is recognized at home and whether it needs a separate registration.
- In plain words: Japanese law says that to decide which country's law applies to a divorce, you first look at whether both spouses have the same national law; if so, that country's law applies. If one spouse is a Japanese national living in Japan, Japanese law applies. So when two foreigners of the same nationality divorce in Japan, their own country's law often applies, and the Japanese procedure does not necessarily mean their country recognizes it.
- Benefit: The Act on General Rules for Application of Laws provides that divorce applies the rules on the effects of marriage mutatis mutandis: if the spouses' national law is the same, that law applies; if there is no common national law but the law of their habitual residence is the same, that law applies; if neither, the law of the place most closely connected with the spouses. However, if one spouse is a Japanese national habitually resident in Japan, Japanese law applies to the divorce
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 法の適用に関する通則法 第二十五条、第二十七条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/418AC0000000078>
- Notes: Why grade B: the rule on applicable law is clear, but whether a particular country recognizes a Japanese divorce procedure has not been checked here. Follow your own embassy's or consulate's guidance. The benefit is rated small because it avoids later disputes over being "still married" in your country of origin. If you have children, also ask about custody and child support at the same time.
