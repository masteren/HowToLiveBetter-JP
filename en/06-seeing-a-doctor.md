# 6. Seeing a doctor: health insurance and lower medical bills

<!-- zh-intro: ec42a7ee -->

This section covers the key things about medical care in Japan: if you are not in a company health insurance plan you must join National Health Insurance yourself; large bills for hospital stays and surgery have a monthly cap; do not go straight to a large hospital without a referral letter; how to find a hospital that speaks Chinese; and, when illness keeps you from work, the sickness allowance, the medical expense deduction, and reduced costs for psychiatric outpatient care. Entries are ordered by cost-effectiveness. Amounts are as of 2026-10-03. The high-cost medical expense benefit was just revised in August 2026 and will change again in August 2027.

### 1. For large hospital or surgery bills, use the high-cost medical expense benefit: your monthly share has a cap, and in hospital you only pay up to the cap at the counter
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 住院、做手术花得多，用高额疗养费：一个月自付有上限，住院时窗口只付到上限 | e1790464 -->
- Cost: Free. Use your My Number Card registered as a health insurance card (マイナ保険証), or get a "limit certificate" (限度額適用認定証) from your insurer in advance, and the counter will only charge up to the cap.
- In plain words: If your out-of-pocket medical costs in one month exceed a cap, insurance pays the excess. For example, take someone under 70 with an annual income of about 3.7 to 5.1 million yen whose medical costs come to 1 million yen. They only pay about 92,940 yen themselves. In hospital, you only pay up to the cap at the counter, with nothing to pay first and claim back. From August 2026 a yearly overall cap was also added.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) states that the high-cost medical expense benefit (高額療養費) keeps out-of-pocket medical costs from becoming too heavy: the part of your share above the monthly limit is repaid by the insurer afterwards. For hospital stays, payment at the counter stops at the limit; since April 2012 the same applies to outpatient care at one medical institution above the limit. The limit depends on income. MHLW's example: for a person under 70 with an annual income of about 3.7 to 5.1 million yen paying 30%, medical costs of 1,000,000 yen give a limit of 85,800 yen plus (1,000,000 yen minus 286,000 yen) times 1%, which is 92,940 yen. For medical costs of 2 million yen it is 102,940 yen. The limit table for August 2026 to July 2027 adds a new yearly cap. If you used the benefit in 3 or more of the past 12 months, the limit is lower from the 4th month
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 厚生労働省「高額療養費制度の概要」患者負担割合及び高額療養費自己負担限度額（令和８年８月～令和９年７月）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001729632.pdf> ; 厚生労働省「高額療養費制度を利用される皆さまへ」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iryouhoken/juuyou/kougakuiryou/index.html>
- Notes: The cap is calculated per calendar month. If you are admitted at the end of one month and discharged early the next, the costs may be split across two months, each counted separately. For the exact amount for each income band, see MHLW's limit table or ask your insurer.

### 2. If illness or injury keeps you from work and your employer stops paying you, claim the health insurance sickness allowance: about two-thirds of your daily pay from day 4, for up to 18 months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 生病或受伤上不了班、公司又不发工资，申请健康保险的伤病津贴：第 4 天起每天约给日薪的三分之二，最长一年半 | fc72d866 -->
- Cost: Free. Fill in the claim form, have your doctor add their opinion, and have your employer fill in your attendance and pay.
- In plain words: If you are in your employer's health insurance and cannot work for 3 days in a row because of treatment, you get paid from day 4. The amount is about two-thirds of your usual daily pay. For the same illness you can receive it for up to 18 months. Days your employer still pays you are not covered; if the pay is lower, the difference is made up.
- Benefit: Health Insurance Act, Article 99: when an insured person cannot work because of medical treatment, the sickness allowance (傷病手当金) is paid from the 4th day after 3 days have passed since they became unable to work. The daily amount is 1/30 of the average standard monthly remuneration over the past 12 consecutive months, times 2/3. For the same illness or injury, it is paid for up to 1 year and 6 months in total from the day payment starts. Health Insurance Act, Article 108: it is not paid for days on which salary is received; if the salary is less than the allowance, the difference is paid. Health Insurance Act, Article 104: a person who was insured continuously for 1 year or more before leaving the job and was already receiving it when leaving can keep receiving it after leaving until it runs out. By this formula, someone with a standard monthly remuneration of 300,000 yen gets about 200,000 yen a month.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 健康保険法 第 99、104、108 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/211AC0000000070>；国民健康保険法 第 58 条第 2 項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/333AC0000000192>
- Notes: Only people in an employer's health insurance (社保) get this. The National Health Insurance Act only says municipalities "may" pay it, and the National Health Insurance that most others are in generally does not. Injuries at work or while commuting do not go through here but through workers' accident compensation; see Section 7, entry 2 (workers' accident compensation). If you plan to quit and return to your country of origin or change jobs, first get the "continue receiving after leaving" rule clear before quitting. The beneficiary is mainly you.

### 3. If you are not in a company health insurance plan, join National Health Insurance at the ward office within 14 days of moving in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 没进公司健康保险的，住进来 14 天内去区役所加入国民健康保险 | d0ce4845 -->
- Cost: Premiums are set each year by income, with reductions for low incomes. The paperwork is one trip to the ward office, usually done together with resident registration.
- In plain words: Foreigners who are registered as residents in Japan and are not in a company health insurance plan must join National Health Insurance (国民健康保険). Once you join, you pay 30% of medical costs; children before primary school and people 70 or over pay 20% in principle. Coverage starts from the day you move in, so if you report late, premiums are still charged back to that day, for up to 2 years. Some municipalities also fine people who do not report on time.
- Benefit: The National Health Insurance Act provides that people with an address in a prefecture are insured under National Health Insurance, except those insured under employment-based plans such as Health Insurance. Among foreigners, those without resident registration and those with certain Designated Activities such as staying for medical treatment are excluded. Eligibility starts from the day you have an address in the area. When eligibility starts, the head of household must notify the municipality within 14 days. Municipalities may by ordinance impose a non-penal fine (過料) of up to 100,000 yen for failure to report or false reports. The right to collect premiums expires after 2 years if not exercised. Out-of-pocket share for medical care: 30% from the first March 31 after age 6 up to the month you turn 70; 20% before that; 20% in principle from 70
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国民健康保険法 第五条、第六条、第七条、第四十二条、第百十条、第百二十七条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/333AC0000000192> ; 国民健康保険法施行規則 第一条、第二条、第三条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/333M50000100053>
- Notes: The benefit is rated large because without insurance a single hospital stay can cost hundreds of thousands of yen. Company health insurance is handled by the company. If you leave a job and do not go straight into a new company, switch from company insurance to National Health Insurance within 14 days. Unpaid premiums count against a permanent residence application; see Section 4, entry 5 (pay taxes, pension and health insurance on time).

### 4. If you and your family spent over 100,000 yen on medical care in a year, file a tax return the next year and claim the medical expense deduction
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 一年里自己和家人看病花的钱超过 10 万日元，第二年自己报税，申请医疗费扣除 | a80791f8 -->
- Cost: Free. Keep a year's receipts for medical care and medicine, and file a final tax return at the tax office once the next year. Employees normally have year-end adjustment done by their company, but this time you file yourself.
- In plain words: Take what you and the family members you share a livelihood with paid for medical care in a year, minus what insurance and compensation paid back. The part above 100,000 yen can be deducted from your income. For lower incomes, the threshold is 5% of income. The deduction is capped at 2 million yen. It comes off your income, so the tax saved is that amount times your tax rate.
- Benefit: Income Tax Act, Article 73: medical expenses paid for yourself, or for a spouse or relatives who "share the same livelihood" (生計を一にする) with you, minus amounts reimbursed by insurance or compensation, can be deducted from income to the extent they exceed 5% of total income for the year. If 5% exceeds 100,000 yen, 100,000 yen is used. The deduction is capped at 2 million yen. Eligible costs are those "ordinarily necessary", such as a doctor's examination and treatment and medicine for treatment.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 所得税法 第 73 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/340AC0000000033>
- Notes: This reduces taxable income; it is not a refund of that amount. For example, an extra 100,000 yen deduction saves about 10,000 yen of income tax for someone at a 10% rate. The benefit is rated medium on the basis of "thousands to tens of thousands of yen". Which costs count follows the National Tax Agency's explanation. For a month with large hospital or surgery bills, first cap your share with the high-cost medical expense benefit; see entry 1 of this section (high-cost medical expense benefit).

### 5. If you need long-term psychiatric outpatient care for depression, anxiety or similar conditions, apply for Medical Care for Services and Supports for Persons with Disabilities, cutting your share from 30% to 10% or less
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 因为抑郁、焦虑这类病要长期看精神科门诊，申请自立支援医疗，自付从三成降到一成以内 | 5db15479 -->
- Cost: Free. Get a medical certificate from your doctor and apply at the counter of your municipality. It can only be used at designated hospitals and pharmacies. The certification has a validity period and must be renewed.
- In plain words: If you need long-term psychiatric outpatient visits and medication for depression, anxiety or similar conditions, this scheme (自立支援医療) lowers what you pay for visits and medicine to at most 10%, instead of the usual 30%. You can also apply if your symptoms have mostly gone and you keep attending to prevent relapse.
- Benefit: Act on Comprehensive Support for Persons with Disabilities, Article 58: for self-reliance support medical care received at a designated medical institution, the person's share is set by the household's ability to pay, up to 10% of the cost. MHLW's explanation of psychiatric outpatient care: it covers mental disorders (including epilepsy) defined in Article 5 of the Mental Health and Welfare Act, with listed conditions including mood disorders (such as depression and bipolar disorder), neurotic and stress-related disorders (such as anxiety), schizophrenia and developmental disorders. People whose symptoms have almost disappeared but who need to keep attending to maintain stability and prevent relapse are also covered. Health Insurance Act, Article 74: people under 70 normally pay 30% of medical costs.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 障害者の日常生活及び社会生活を総合的に支援するための法律 第 58 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/417AC0000000123>；厚生労働省. 自立支援医療（精神通院医療）の概要. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/bunya/shougaihoken/jiritsu/seishin.html>；健康保険法 第 74 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/211AC0000000070>
- Notes: It covers outpatient care only, not hospital stays. The monthly cap on your share depends on income bands; ask at the municipal counter. If you feel you cannot go on, call a helpline first; see Section 1, entry 2 (Yorisoi Hotline). The beneficiary is you or your family.

### 6. If you fall ill or get injured while visiting your country of origin and see a doctor, keep the receipts and itemized treatment record, and after returning to Japan claim part of it back from health insurance as "overseas medical expenses"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 回原籍国探亲时急病受伤看了医生，留好收据和诊疗明细，回日本后用「海外疗养费」从健康保险报回一部分 | d65db033 -->
- Cost: Free. Ask the local hospital for an itemized record of treatment and the original receipts. After returning to Japan, submit them with proof of travel such as your passport to your health insurance counter.
- In plain words: People with an address in Japan who travel abroad for a short time and, because of sudden illness or injury, have to see a doctor there can claim overseas medical expenses (海外療養費) after returning. The amount is calculated as if you had the same treatment in Japan, and you get it back minus your usual share. Trips made specifically for treatment, non-urgent surgery and dental fillings, and treatments not covered by Japanese insurance are not paid. You must claim within 2 years.
- Benefit: Osaka City states: if, while abroad, you have no choice but to be treated at a medical institution outside Japan because of sudden illness or injury, you are paid the amount calculated on the basis of treatment in Japan (or the amount actually paid abroad, if lower), minus a share. Overseas medical expenses apply to people with an address in Japan travelling abroad for a short time, not to people living abroad long-term; going abroad for the purpose of treatment does not count; the person must claim after returning. Examples of what is not paid: non-urgent visits, planned visits and hospital stays, ongoing treatment of chronic illness, treatment of tooth decay, non-urgent surgery, treatments not covered by insurance in Japan (cosmetic surgery, dental implants, orthodontics and the like), health checkups, vaccinations, medicines not approved in Japan, ambulance fees, and private room charges. Article 110 of the National Health Insurance Act provides that the right to receive insurance benefits expires if not exercised within 2 years from when it could be exercised
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 大阪市「療養費・移送費・海外療養費の支給」（accessed 2026-10-04）. <https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/fukushi/page/0000369686.html>；国民健康保険法 第 110 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/333AC0000000192>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-04. Graded B because the wording comes from Osaka City's National Health Insurance page, and the required documents vary slightly between insurers. Company health insurance has the same scheme; ask your company or health insurance society. Since it is calculated on Japanese standards, if medical care in your country of origin is more expensive than in Japan, only part comes back. For translation requirements and forms, follow your own insurer's page. The benefit is rated medium because one emergency hospital stay may bring back tens of thousands of yen.

### 7. For ordinary illness go to a nearby clinic first; going straight to a large hospital without a referral letter costs 7,000 yen or more extra
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 普通的病先去附近的诊所，别不带介绍信直接去大医院，要多付 7,000 日元以上 | a03f5134 -->
- Cost: Free. Go to a nearby clinic first, and the doctor will write a referral letter if needed.
- In plain words: Going to a large hospital such as a university hospital for a first visit without a referral letter costs an extra 7,000 yen or more on top of your 30% share. If, on a follow-up visit, the hospital has already said you can go back to a smaller clinic but you keep coming, it is an extra 3,000 yen or more. See a nearby clinic first, and have the doctor write a referral letter when needed.
- Benefit: MHLW states that advanced treatment hospitals, regional medical support hospitals with 200 or more general beds, and referral-focused medical institutions with 200 or more general beds may charge outpatients who come without a referral letter a "special fee" on top of their share (30% and so on). From October 2022, it is 7,000 yen or more for a first medical visit and 3,000 yen or more for a follow-up medical visit; for dental care, 5,000 yen or more for a first visit and 1,900 yen or more for a follow-up. Medical institutions may not charge this special fee to emergency patients and the like. The follow-up fee applies to patients who keep coming even though the hospital has already written them a referral back to another medical institution. The fiscal 2026 revision of medical fees did not change these amounts
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 厚生労働省「患者のみなさまへ 医療機関の機能・役割に応じた適切な受診を」（令和４年 10 月）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12400000/000963828.pdf> ; 厚生労働省「令和８年度診療報酬改定 ７.外来医療の機能分化・強化等」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12400000/001681336.pdf>
- Notes: The benefit is rated small at a few thousand yen per visit. For which nearby clinics can use foreign languages, see entry 8 (finding a hospital that speaks Chinese).

### 8. To find a hospital that speaks Chinese, check the MHLW and Japan Tourism Agency list of medical institutions for foreign patients
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
<!-- zh: 找能说中文的医院，查厚生劳动省和观光厅的外国患者医疗机构名单 | bde64546 -->
- Cost: Free. Search by area, language and department on the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) website, or download MHLW's Excel list.
- In plain words: MHLW and the Japan Tourism Agency publish a list of medical institutions that accept foreign patients, chosen by each prefecture. The JNTO website is available in Chinese and can be searched by address, language spoken and department. Every prefecture has at least one institution that can accept foreign emergency patients who need hospitalization.
- Benefit: MHLW states that, together with the Japan Tourism Agency, it publishes a "list of medical institutions accepting foreign patients"; the listed institutions are chosen by each prefecture, and the list is published in Excel format. The list has two categories: the first is institutions that can accept foreign emergency patients needing hospitalization, at least one per prefecture; the second is institutions that can accept foreign patients, including clinics and dental clinics, at least one per secondary medical area. The JNTO website also lists these institutions, in Japanese, English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese and Korean, searchable by location, available languages, department and so on, with advice on how to see a doctor
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 厚生労働省「「外国人患者を受け入れる医療機関の情報を取りまとめたリスト」について」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_05774.html> ; 厚生労働省「医療機関リストの解説」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10800000/000957980.pdf>
- Notes: Why grade B: the list is official, but there is no figure for how much time it saves. The list says it is for visitors to Japan, but foreigners living in Japan can use it too. Information on the JNTO site is updated later than the Excel list; when they differ, go by the Excel list.
