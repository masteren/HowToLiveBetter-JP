# 26. Driving and driver's licenses

<!-- zh-intro: c1de48bf -->

This section covers where driving in Japan most easily goes wrong: how long your licence can be used and how to switch it, which insurance to buy, and what to do after an accident or after drinking. The main measure is personal freedom; the insurance entries are about money. Entries are ordered by cost-effectiveness. For the medical evidence on not speeding and not drink-driving, see Section 1, entry 5 (do not speed or drink-drive); for cycling rules, see Section 1, entry 25 (do not look at your phone while cycling).

### 1. After a traffic accident, stop immediately, help the injured and call the police, even for a minor scrape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 出了交通事故，马上停车救人、报警，小剐蹭也要报 | 8e9f0481 -->
- Cost: Free. You have to wait at the scene for the police, losing an hour or two.
- In plain words: After an accident, the driver must stop at once, help the injured and prevent further danger, then report to the police, calling them at once if none are at the scene. Driving off after injuring someone carries up to 10 years' imprisonment. Not reporting an accident with no injuries is also punished.
- Benefit: Road Traffic Act, Article 72: when a traffic accident occurs, the driver and crew must stop at once, help the injured and take measures to prevent danger on the road. The driver must report to a police officer at the scene; if no police officer is at the scene, they must at once report to the nearest police station the time, place, number of dead and injured, damage, and measures taken. Road Traffic Act, Article 117: failing to help when someone is killed or injured is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years or a fine of up to 500,000 yen; if the death or injury was caused by your own driving, by imprisonment of up to 10 years or a fine of up to 1 million yen. Road Traffic Act, Article 119: failing to report is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 months or a fine of up to 50,000 yen.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 道路交通法 第 72、117、119 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/335AC0000000105>
- Notes: The duty to report applies to accidents of any size, including those that only damage property. If you settle privately on the spot without calling the police, and the other side later changes their mind or the injury worsens, you will have no way to prove what happened. If someone collapses and is not breathing, first follow Section 3, entry 15 (someone collapses and is not breathing). The beneficiaries are you and the other party.

### 2. An international driving permit or foreign licence can only be used for 1 year from the day you enter Japan; driving after that is driving without a licence
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 国际驾照和外国驾照从入境那天起只能开 1 年，过期再开就是无照驾驶 | 08d31f39 -->
- Cost: Free. Remember the day you entered Japan, and switch to a Japanese licence before the year is up.
- In plain words: With an international driving permit, or a foreign licence with a designated Japanese translation, you can only drive for one year from the day you enter. An international permit also expires one year after issue, and whichever comes first applies. Driving after it expires counts as driving without a licence, with up to 3 years' imprisonment. For residents of Japan who go abroad and return within 3 months, the year does not restart.
- Benefit: Road Traffic Act, Article 107-2: holders of an international driving permit in the format of the Geneva Convention (1949 Convention on Road Traffic), or of a licence from a country or region designated by cabinet order together with a Japanese translation by a designated body, may drive for 1 year from the day of entry. For people registered in the Basic Resident Register who leave Japan and re-enter within 3 months, that entry does not restart the count. The National Police Agency explains: the period you can drive is the shorter of 1 year from entry and the validity of the permit, and an international driving permit is valid for 1 year from issue. Road Traffic Act, Article 117-2-2: driving without a licence is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 500,000 yen.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 道路交通法 第 107 条の 2、117 条の 2 の 2. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/335AC0000000105>；警察庁. 外国の運転免許をお持ちの方. <https://www.npa.go.jp/policies/application/license_renewal/have_DL_issed_another_country.html>
- Notes: Only international permits in the 1949 Geneva Convention format are accepted. If your country does not issue this kind of international permit, that route is closed, and you need to switch to a Japanese licence; see entry 7 of this section (switching to a Japanese licence). People living in Japan who often go back to their country of origin are the most likely to miscount this year. The beneficiary is you.

### 3. Do not drive after drinking, do not lend your car to someone who has been drinking, and do not ride in a car they drive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 喝了酒不开车，也别把车借给喝了酒的人、别坐他开的车 | 8458686d -->
- Cost: Free. When drinking, take a taxi or train, or find someone who has not been drinking to drive.
- In plain words: Driving while drunk carries up to 5 years' imprisonment, and driving over the alcohol limit up to 3 years. Lending your car to someone who has been drinking, offering drinks to someone who will drive, and getting a driver you know has been drinking to take you home are all also illegal.
- Benefit: Road Traffic Act, Article 65: no one may drive after drinking; no one may provide a vehicle to someone likely to drive after drinking; no one may provide or offer alcohol to someone likely to drive after drinking; no one may, knowing the driver has been drinking, ask to be driven and ride along. Road Traffic Act, Article 117-2: driving while intoxicated is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years or a fine of up to 1 million yen. Road Traffic Act, Article 117-2-2: driving with alcohol at or above the level set by cabinet order is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 500,000 yen; providing a vehicle, providing alcohol and riding along are also punished under this article in specified cases.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 道路交通法 第 65、117 条の 2、117 条の 2 の 2. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/335AC0000000105>
- Notes: A criminal sentence affects your status of residence; see Section 2, entry 5 (a sentence can get you deported). Which penalty applies to passengers and people who provided the vehicle depends on how drunk the driver was, and the provisions draw very fine lines. The simplest approach is not to touch a drop. The beneficiaries are you and the people in the car.

### 4. If you cycle, first check whether you have personal liability insurance: Tokyo and other areas require it by ordinance, and compensation for injuring someone can be large
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 骑自行车先查自己有没有个人赔偿责任保险：东京等地条例规定必须加入，撞伤人的赔偿可能很大 | 08941a29 -->
- Cost: First look through the insurance you already have; car insurance, fire insurance, credit cards and company group insurance often include a personal liability rider. If none do, buy a policy separately.
- In plain words: Since April 2020, a Tokyo ordinance has required cyclists to have insurance that compensates others for injury or death. Parents arrange it for minors, and companies for bicycles used for work. The "personal liability insurance" attached to many policies counts, so check whether you already have it before buying a duplicate.
- Benefit: The Metropolitan Police Department states: from April 1, 2020, Tokyo amended the "Tokyo Metropolitan Ordinance on Promoting Safe and Proper Use of Bicycles", making "bicycle damage liability insurance or mutual aid" that can compensate for harm to others in a cycling accident mandatory. Cyclists must have insurance that compensates for harm to others' life or body; if the cyclist is a minor, the guardian must arrange it; businesses must arrange it for bicycles used for work; rental businesses must also have it. Insurance to check includes riders on car, fire and accident insurance, insurance attached to credit cards, company group insurance and group insurance offered through schools, looking for "personal liability insurance" (個人賠償責任保険). Personal liability insurance does not cover accidents while cycling for work
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 警視庁「自転車事故に備える保険等の加入義務」（updated 2026-04-03）. <https://www.keishicho.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/kotsu/jikoboshi/bicycle/menu/bicycle_insurance.html>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-04. Graded B because these are each prefecture's own ordinances; whether places outside Tokyo have a duty and how it is defined, check your prefecture's page. For the coverage amount, check that "bodily injury liability" is enough; the Metropolitan Police Department advises checking that the coverage is sufficient. For the penalty for using a phone while cycling, see entry 6 of this section (holding a phone). The benefit is rated large because of the amounts that may be owed for injuring someone; this is the book's judgment, without citing specific judgments.

### 5. If you own a car, buy voluntary insurance on top of compulsory automobile liability insurance: the compulsory insurance pays at most 30 million yen for a death and only 1.2 million for an injury
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 自己有车，除了强制的自赔责保险，再买任意保险：自赔责死亡最多赔 3000 万日元，受伤只到 120 万 | 4eda3df9 -->
- Cost: Voluntary insurance (任意保険) costs a premium every year, depending on the car, your age and the coverage. Comparing several insurers takes an hour or two.
- In plain words: A car without compulsory automobile liability insurance (自賠責保険) may not be driven, and doing so carries up to a year's imprisonment. The compulsory insurance only pays for people you injure or kill, at most 30 million yen for a death and 1.2 million yen for an injury. Paying more for hitting someone, or damaging someone else's car, comes out of your own pocket, so you also need voluntary insurance.
- Benefit: Automobile Liability Security Act, Article 5: a car without a compulsory liability insurance or mutual aid contract may not be driven on the road. Automobile Liability Security Act, Article 86-3: violations are punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 500,000 yen. Automobile Liability Security Act, Article 3: it covers harm to others' life or body caused by driving. Under the enforcement order of the Automobile Liability Security Act, the compulsory insurance amount is 30 million yen per person for death and 1.2 million yen for injury, with separate amounts by grade for after-effects requiring care.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 自動車損害賠償保障法 第 3、5、86 条の 3. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/330AC0000000097>；自動車損害賠償保障法施行令 第 2 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/330CO0000000286>
- Notes: Compensation above the compulsory insurance must be paid by you. How much is awarded depends on the case, so no figure is given here. What voluntary insurance covers and how much differs by insurer; at the least, check the coverage amounts for bodily injury and property damage. The beneficiaries are you and the person hit.

### 6. Holding a phone to talk or stare at it while driving or cycling is illegal, with up to 1 year if it causes danger
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 开车、骑自行车时手里拿着手机打电话或盯着看就违法，造成危险的最重判 1 年 | 57fbf5c6 -->
- Cost: Free. Set the navigation before setting off and put the phone in a holder. To make a call, stop first.
- In plain words: When driving a car or riding a motorcycle or bicycle, talking on a hand-held phone or staring at a phone screen in your hand while not stopped is illegal. The maximum is imprisonment of up to 6 months or a fine of up to 100,000 yen. If it causes traffic danger, the maximum is imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 300,000 yen. Unavoidable calls, such as to help the injured, are an exception.
- Benefit: Road Traffic Act, Article 71, item 5-5 provides: when driving a car, motorized bicycle or bicycle, except while stopped, you may not use a mobile phone or other wireless calling device that must be held in the hand to make calls (except unavoidable calls while moving to help the sick or injured or maintain public safety), nor hold an image display device brought into the vehicle and stare at its screen. Road Traffic Act, Article 118, paragraph 1, item 4 provides that violations of the above are punishable by imprisonment of up to 6 months or a fine of up to 100,000 yen. Road Traffic Act, Article 117-4, item 2 provides that violating the above and thereby causing traffic danger on the road is punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 300,000 yen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 道路交通法 第 71 条、第 117 条の 4、第 118 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/335AC0000000105>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. Such violations on a bicycle can lead to a ticket from April 2026; see Section 1, entry 25 (do not look at your phone while cycling). Navigation fixed in a holder and not held in the hand is not "holding and staring", but staring at it continuously while driving is still dangerous. For what imprisonment means for your residence, see Section 2, entry 5 (a sentence can get you deported). The benefit is rated medium because it avoids criminal penalties.

### 7. If you settle down and will drive long-term, switch your foreign licence to a Japanese one: you must prove you lived 3 months in the issuing country after getting it, bring your residence record, and go in person
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
<!-- zh: 住下来要长期开车，就把外国驾照换成日本驾照：要证明拿证后在发证国住满 3 个月，要带住民票，必须本人去 | e0fa9aa3 -->
- Cost: You pay fees, and need a Japanese translation from your licence's issuing authority, consulate or a designated body. You go in person to the driver's licence center, possibly several times.
- In plain words: A foreign licence can be switched to a Japanese one with parts of the test waived (外免切替). There are three conditions. You must have lived a total of 3 months in the issuing country after getting the licence, shown by a passport with entry and exit stamps. You need a residence record (住民票), so short-term visitors in principle cannot do it. The translation must be made by the issuing authority, a consulate or a designated body; a translation you arrange yourself does not count. You must go in person, with no proxy.
- Benefit: Road Traffic Act, Article 97-2, paragraph 3: for people with a foreign licence, once the public safety commission confirms there is no problem with their driving, parts of the licence test may be waived. The National Police Agency explains: the condition is a total stay of 3 months or more in that country after obtaining the licence, proved by documents such as a passport with entry and exit stamps. Foreigners not covered by the Basic Resident Registration Act cannot in principle apply, except for statutory exceptions. You must submit a residence record stating your status of residence and other items, your home-country licence (an international permit is not accepted), and a Japanese translation by a person set by cabinet order. No proxy applications.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 道路交通法 第 97 条の 2 第 3 項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/335AC0000000105>；警察庁. 外国の運転免許をお持ちの方. <https://www.npa.go.jp/policies/application/license_renewal/have_DL_issed_another_country.html>
- Notes: What is waived is the written and practical test, so you do not have to start again from driving school. There is no single figure for the time saved, so the benefit is rated medium by judgment. The public safety commission checks your knowledge and skills, and if you do not pass you must book again. How to book and what is checked differ by prefecture; ask the driver's licence center where you live. The beneficiary is you.
