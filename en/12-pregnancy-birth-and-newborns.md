# 12. Pregnancy, childbirth and newborns: subsidies, birth registration and your child's residence

<!-- zh-intro: 8e7734e6 -->

This section covers the money foreigners can claim and the procedures that must be done on time when getting pregnant and giving birth in Japan. If the child is not a Japanese national, a separate status of residence must be applied for after birth, with a very short deadline. Amounts are as of 2026-10-03. The medical entries on pregnancy and newborns are carried over from the original book's sections "pregnancy and childbirth" and "caring for a newborn", with the research figures unchanged and Chinese prices and systems replaced by Japanese ones.

### 1. Once your pregnancy is confirmed, file a pregnancy notification at the ward office to get the Maternal and Child Health Handbook and prenatal check-up vouchers; at least 14 check-ups are publicly subsidized
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 确认怀孕后去区役所交妊娠届，领母子健康手帐和孕检补助券，孕检公费补 14 次以上 | 28655609 -->
- Cost: Free. Once a clinic confirms the pregnancy, file a pregnancy notification (妊娠届) with the municipality where you live.
- In plain words: If you become pregnant in Japan, file a pregnancy notification at the ward office, and you will get the Maternal and Child Health Handbook (母子健康手帳) and vouchers for prenatal check-ups. Every municipality in the country subsidizes at least 14 check-ups, averaging over 100,000 yen per person.
- Benefit: A Children and Families Agency survey states that as of April 1, 2023, all 1,741 municipalities subsidized prenatal health check-ups at least 14 times; the public subsidy per pregnant woman averaged 108,481 yen nationwide (excluding 78 municipalities that did not state amounts)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: こども家庭庁「妊婦健康診査の公費負担の状況について（令和５年４月１日現在）」. <https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/d4a9b67b-acbd-4e2a-a27a-7e8f2d6106dd/b82ceb26/20240422_policies_boshihoken_tsuuchi_2024_29.pdf>
- Notes: The benefit is rated large because prenatal check-ups cost around 100,000 yen. The exact amounts and items subsidized differ by municipality. Ask at the counter whether a foreign-language version of the Maternal and Child Health Handbook is available.

### 2. When you give birth, claim the childbirth and childcare lump-sum allowance, in principle 500,000 yen per child; with direct payment you do not pay it up front
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 生孩子领出产育儿一时金，每个孩子原则上 50 万日元，用直接支付制度就不用先垫 | 34e0f717 -->
- Cost: Free. Be in health insurance (company insurance or National Health Insurance both count). When admitted, sign the hospital's consent form for the direct payment system.
- In plain words: People enrolled in public medical insurance receive in principle 500,000 yen per child when they give birth. With the direct payment system, the insurer pays the hospital directly, and you only pay the excess at the counter. It was raised from 420,000 yen to 500,000 yen in April 2023.
- Benefit: MHLW states that the childbirth and childcare lump-sum allowance (出産育児一時金) is a system under which, when someone enrolled in public medical insurance gives birth, their insurer pays in principle 500,000 yen per child. From April 2023 it was raised from in principle 420,000 yen to in principle 500,000 yen, the first increase in 13 years. Under the "direct payment system", the insurer pays the lump sum directly to the medical institution, and at the counter you only pay the part of the childbirth costs above the lump sum
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 厚生労働省「出産育児一時金等について」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iryouhoken/shussan/index.html>
- Notes: People not enrolled in National Health Insurance cannot get it, so join as soon as you move in; see Section 6, entry 3 (join National Health Insurance). If childbirth costs are below 500,000 yen, you can claim the difference from your insurer. If you give birth in your country of origin, ask your insurer whether and how you can claim.

### 3. Apply for the child allowance within 15 days of the day after birth; months you apply late for are not paid
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 出生次日起 15 天内申请儿童补贴，晚了的月份领不到 | a7210858 -->
- Cost: Free. Within 15 days from the day after birth, apply at the municipality of your current address, bringing your bank account details and other documents. If you give birth in your country of origin or elsewhere, still apply to your current municipality.
- In plain words: Children living in Japan, of any nationality, receive the child allowance (児童手当) until the year they finish high school, regardless of parents' income. Under 3, 15,000 yen a month; from 3 through high school, 10,000 yen a month. From the third child, 30,000 yen a month. In principle it is paid from the month after you apply, and months you apply late for are not paid.
- Benefit: The Children and Families Agency states that from October 2024 the child allowance was drastically expanded: the income limit was abolished; eligibility was extended to high school age, meaning children with an address in Japan until the end of the first fiscal year after they turn 18; the amount from the third child was raised to 30,000 yen; and payment changed to every two months (even months). Monthly amounts: under 3, 15,000 yen for the first and second child, 30,000 yen from the third; from 3 to high school age, 10,000 yen for the first and second child, 30,000 yen from the third. In principle it is paid from the month after the application month; if the birth or move-in date is near the end of a month and the application falls in the next month but within 15 days from the day after, it is paid from the application month. If you apply late, in principle you cannot receive the late months. When a child is born, apply to the municipality of your current address within 15 days from the day after birth; if the mother is temporarily away, such as giving birth in her home town, still apply to your current municipality
- Evidence: A
- Sources: こども家庭庁「児童手当制度のご案内」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.cfa.go.jp/policies/kokoseido/jidouteate/annai> ; こども家庭庁「児童手当制度の概要」. <https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/e2f6f91e-392c-4cd1-a3fc-ff8f15b94e76/db1c9e30/20250401_policies_jyouhouseisaku_Jidoteate-hyojunka_r05_e2f6f91e_03.pdf>
- Notes: If you move to another municipality, you must apply again within 15 days from the day after moving in. The benefit is rated large at over 100,000 yen a year. The child must have an address in Japan; if they go to live in your country of origin for a long time, they may no longer qualify; ask your municipality.

### 4. Memorize this "go to hospital now" list; it applies during pregnancy and for a year after birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 背下这张「立刻去医院」的清单，孕期和产后一年内都算数 | 750f0a52 -->
- Cost: Free. Five minutes to memorize it, and ideally the people you live with should know it too.
- In plain words: If any of these appears, go to hospital that day; do not wait overnight at home. A headache that will not go away or gets worse, changes in vision, or a fever of 38 °C. Severe swelling of the hands or face, trouble breathing, chest pain or a racing heart. Severe belly pain that does not ease, the baby's movements stopping or clearly slowing, vaginal bleeding or fluid leaking. One arm or leg swollen and painful, or thoughts of harming yourself or the baby. This applies for a year after birth too.
- Benefit: The urgent maternal warning signs listed by the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention are: a headache that won't go away or gets worse, dizziness or fainting, changes in vision, a temperature of 38 °C or higher; extreme swelling of the hands or face, thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, trouble breathing, chest pain or a fast-beating heart; severe nausea and vomiting, severe belly pain that doesn't go away, the baby's movements stopping or slowing during pregnancy; vaginal bleeding or fluid leaking during pregnancy, abnormal vaginal bleeding or discharge after birth; severe swelling, redness or pain in one arm or one leg. These problems can occur up to a year after birth; it is not "fine once the first month is over"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Notes: The point of this list is to separate "bear it a bit longer" from "must go now". A headache plus blurred vision, plus upper belly pain, points to pre-eclampsia (a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy). One calf swollen and painful points to deep vein thrombosis. According to the World Health Organization, postpartum haemorrhage can kill a healthy woman within hours. If any item appears, go straight to hospital or call 119; do not wait overnight at home first.

### 5. File the birth notification within 14 days of birth, and if the child is not a Japanese national, apply to immigration for the child's status of residence within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 孩子出生后 14 天内交出生届，不是日本籍的，30 天内向入管申请孩子的在留资格 | 8b8c441a -->
- Cost: Free. File the birth notification at the ward office with the hospital's birth certificate. Then take documents such as the certificate of acceptance of the birth notification to immigration and apply for the child's status of residence.
- In plain words: If both parents are foreigners, a child born in Japan is not a Japanese national. A foreigner's child born in Japan still needs a birth notification (出生届) within 14 days. After birth the child can only stay 60 days without a status, and to keep living here you must apply to immigration for a status of residence within 30 days. If the child leaves Japan within 60 days, you need not apply.
- Benefit: The Family Register Act provides that a birth notification must be filed within 14 days. The Ministry of Justice states that foreigners have no family register, but births and deaths in Japan fall under the Family Register Act, and a birth notification must be filed with the local municipality; the notification is kept for 10 years, and a certificate of acceptance can be requested when proof is needed. The Immigration Act provides that a foreigner who is in Japan without going through landing procedures because of birth or similar may stay without a status of residence for up to 60 days from birth; to stay beyond that, they must apply to the Minister of Justice for a status of residence within 30 days of birth. The Nationality Act provides that a child is a Japanese national if the father or mother is a Japanese national at the time of birth; a child born in Japan whose parents are both unknown or stateless is also a Japanese national
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 戸籍法 第四十九条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000224> ; 法務省「国際結婚、海外での出生等に関する戸籍Ｑ＆Ａ」. <https://www.moj.go.jp/MINJI/minji15.html> ; 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十二条の二第一項・第二項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 国籍法 第二条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/325AC0000000147>
- Notes: The child's passport and birth registration in the country of origin are done at that country's embassy or consulate; follow their guidance on deadlines and documents. If one parent is Japanese, the child is a Japanese national from birth and needs no status of residence.

### 6. Make up formula with water of 70 °C or more, let it cool before feeding, and throw away leftovers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 冲奶粉用 70 ℃ 以上的水，冲好放凉再喂，喝剩的倒掉 | 84511d58 -->
- Cost: Free. Wait a few extra minutes each time for the milk to cool. The hard part is waiting for the water to cool before making it up even when the baby is crying with hunger.
- In plain words: Powdered formula is not sterile and may carry a bacterium called Cronobacter sakazakii. When newborns are infected, reported death rates range from 40% to 80%. Pour the hot water into the bottle first, wait until it cools to about 73.8 °C, then add the powder. The milk is then still above 70 °C, which kills this bacterium down to less than one hundred-thousandth of what was there. Let it cool before feeding, and throw away leftovers.
- Benefit: Powdered formula is not sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii. Reported death rates after infection in newborns are 40% to 80%. Laboratory studies show that if hot water is poured into the bottle first and powder is added when the water cools to about 73.8 °C, the milk is in most cases still above 70 °C. This reduces the bacterium by more than 5 log units, to less than one hundred-thousandth of the original
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- Notes: The 70 °C threshold comes from public health guidelines. Beary and colleagues' own words are "public health guidance for caregivers of high-risk infants recommends reconstituting powdered infant formula with water heated to at least 70 °C (158 °F) to inactivate microorganisms". Premature babies, low-birth-weight babies and babies under 2 months are at highest risk. Do not use water that has sat in a thermos all day. And do not leave made-up formula at room temperature waiting for the next feed.

### 7. If a baby under 3 months has a temperature of 38 °C, go straight to hospital; do not watch and wait at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 不满 3 个月的婴儿体温到 38 ℃ 就直接去医院，不在家观察 | a76753b2 -->
- Cost: One emergency visit, even at night.
- In plain words: If your baby is under 3 months and their temperature reaches 38 °C, go straight to hospital. Do not watch at home, and do not give fever medicine first and wait. At this age even a serious bacterial infection may show only as fever. So "they seem fine" is no reason to wait at home. Babies less than 8 days old are not even covered by the guidelines, which is all the more reason to go at once.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics produced a guideline specifically for well-appearing full-term infants aged 8 to 60 days. It defines fever as "a temperature ≥38.0 °C". It divides infants into three bands, 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days and 29 to 60 days, with a care pathway for each. Infants under 8 days are not even within the guideline's scope, meaning that at this stage they need medical care immediately
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>
- Notes: At this age, a serious bacterial infection may show only as fever. So a baby looking "alert enough" is no reason to watch at home. Do not give fever medicine first and wait either. If you do not know which hospital to go to at night, call the children's emergency advice line; see Section 3, entry 39 (call #8000 for children).

### 8. If your baby has severe eczema or egg allergy, do not avoid peanuts; introduce them early under a doctor's guidance, but never give whole peanuts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 孩子有严重湿疹或鸡蛋过敏，别躲着花生，按医生指导早点加上，但绝不能喂整粒 | 52b30264 -->
- Cost: A jar of peanut butter is cheap. Feeding it a few times a week is easy. Before starting, take your baby to a doctor once for an assessment.
- In plain words: Babies with severe eczema or egg allergy who ate peanut regularly from 4 to 11 months had only a 1.9% peanut allergy rate at age 5, versus 13.7% for those who avoided it completely. Among babies whose skin test already showed some reaction, 10.6% of those who ate it were allergic, versus 35.3% who avoided it. See a doctor before starting. Never give whole peanuts; they can choke the airway.
- Benefit: The UK LEAP randomized trial. 640 infants were enrolled with severe eczema, egg allergy or both. They were 4 to 11 months old at enrolment. They were randomized into two groups, one eating peanut products regularly and one avoiding them completely, and peanut allergy rates were measured at 60 months. Among 530 with a negative peanut skin test at enrolment, 13.7% of the avoidance group and 1.9% of the eating group were allergic (P<0.001). Among 98 with a weakly positive skin test at enrolment, 35.3% of the avoidance group and 10.6% of the eating group (P=0.004). Serious adverse events did not differ between groups.
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Du Toit G, Roberts G, Sayre PH, et al. (2015). Randomized trial of peanut consumption in infants at risk for peanut allergy. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(9), 803-813. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414850>；Perkin MR, et al. (2016). Randomized Trial of Introduction of Allergenic Foods in Breast-Fed Infants. New England Journal of Medicine, 374(18), 1733-1743. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1514210> (the EAT trial of general infants in the notes)
- Notes: **Only give it as thinned peanut butter or peanut powder mixed into baby food, never whole peanuts.** For what to do if someone chokes, see Section 3, entry 4 (someone is choking and cannot speak). This only applies to high-risk babies: those with severe eczema or already allergic to egg. **You must see a doctor for an assessment before starting; do not try it at home on your own.** The LEAP trial skin-tested every baby before enrolment. Babies with a skin test reaction over 4 mm were excluded from the trial and not given peanut. Evidence for ordinary babies is much weaker. In another trial of 1,162 ordinary breastfed babies, counted by assigned group, food allergy was 5.6% in the early introduction group and 7.1% in the standard group, not a significant difference (P=0.32). It was only significant among the children who actually followed through (2.4% vs 7.3%). This way of counting tends to exaggerate the effect; the authors themselves say efficacy was not shown by assigned group, but that early introduction was safe. When and how to start, follow your doctor.

### 9. Watch for these newborn jaundice signs: yellow within 24 hours of birth, getting more yellow, hard to wake and not feeding, or pale stools; go to hospital that day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 盯住新生儿黄疸的几个信号：出生 24 小时内就黄、越来越黄、叫不醒不吃奶、大便发白，就当天去医院 | 57df8595 -->
- Cost: One doctor's visit and a bilirubin test. If phototherapy is needed, it is usually about two days in hospital. The hard part is making the trip when the baby looks fine.
- In plain words: Over 80% of newborns turn a bit yellow, and most clear on their own. If they are yellow less than 24 hours after birth, or getting more yellow, unusually sleepy and hard to wake, not feeding, or floppy or stiff, go to hospital that day. Pale stools may mean biliary atresia, which also needs checking soon. The Maternal and Child Health Handbook contains a stool colour card; if the stool matches colours 1 to 3, take a sample to the doctor.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics' 2022 guideline says "more than 80% of newborn infants will have some degree of jaundice". Bilirubin that is too high causes acute bilirubin encephalopathy and kernicterus. The guideline calls kernicterus "a permanent disabling neurologic condition". Its features include cerebral palsy with involuntary writhing of the limbs, inability to look upward, dental enamel defects of the baby teeth and hearing loss. The guideline lists "jaundice within the first 24 hours after birth" as a risk factor for developing severe jaundice. The UK National Health Service (NHS) gives parents two levels. The first means calling emergency services or going straight to A&E. This is when the baby has jaundice and is also unusually sleepy or hard to wake, not feeding, floppy or stiff, or having fits. A baby with jaundice less than 24 hours after birth also falls into this level. The second means seeing a doctor soon. This is when, more than 24 hours after birth, the jaundice is not improving or is getting worse, the urine is dark yellow or brown, or the stools are pale cream. Globally, an estimated 480,000 newborns had Rh haemolytic disease (from blood group incompatibility between mother and baby) or extreme hyperbilirubinaemia in 2010. Of these, 24% died and 13% developed kernicterus. Of children surviving kernicterus, over 83% had one or more impairments. Pale stools may mean biliary atresia, for which the Kasai operation within 60 days gives a clearly better outlook. From 2004 Taiwan printed a stool colour card in every newborn's child health booklet. The proportion operated on within 60 days rose from 60% in 2004 to 74.3% in 2005. In another Taiwanese nationwide study, after the card was introduced, mortality of children with biliary atresia fell from 26.2% to 15.9%. This is a before-and-after comparison, not a randomized trial. Japan's Maternal and Child Health Handbook also has a stool colour card. The guide issued by the Children and Families Agency states: compare and record the number at 2 weeks, 1 month and between 1 and 4 months after birth; if it matches colours 1 to 3, take that stool to the doctor who does the 1-month check-up as soon as possible
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Kemper AR, Newman TB, Slaughter JL, et al. (2022). Clinical Practice Guideline Revision: Management of Hyperbilirubinemia in the Newborn Infant 35 or More Weeks of Gestation. Pediatrics, 150(3), e2022058859. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-058859>；NHS. Jaundice in babies (reviewed 2026-03-24). <https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/jaundice-newborn/>；NICE (2010, revised 2023). Jaundice in newborn babies under 28 days (CG98), 1.2.6, 1.7.1. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg98/chapter/Recommendations>；Bhutani VK, Zipursky A, Blencowe H, et al. (2013). Neonatal hyperbilirubinemia and Rhesus disease of the newborn: incidence and impairment estimates for 2010 at regional and global levels. Pediatric Research, 74(Suppl 1), 86-100. <https://doi.org/10.1038/pr.2013.208>；Hsiao CH, Chang MH, Chen HL, et al. (2008). Universal screening for biliary atresia using an infant stool color card in Taiwan. Hepatology, 47(4), 1233-1240. <https://doi.org/10.1002/hep.22182>；Lee M, Chen SC, Yang HY, et al. (2016). Infant Stool Color Card Screening Helps Reduce the Hospitalization Rate and Mortality of Biliary Atresia: A 14-Year Nationwide Cohort Study in Taiwan. Medicine, 95(12), e3166. <https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000003166>；こども家庭庁「胆道閉鎖症早期発見のための便色カード活用マニュアル」. <https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/909390f5-d0c0-47b9-9b9e-c343b88bde66/aa6c5af7/20230401_policies_boshihoken_techou_09.pdf>
- Notes: Phototherapy shines a special light on the baby's skin to turn bilirubin into a form that is easier to excrete. Do not substitute sunbathing at home: the American Academy of Pediatrics says sunlight is hard to use safely without sunburn, so it is not recommended as treatment. Also do not give water or glucose water to clear jaundice; the guideline explicitly does not recommend this either. Judging the depth of yellow by eye does not estimate bilirubin accurately. The UK NICE guideline says not to rely on the eye alone; if unsure, get a test. Babies born before 38 weeks, whose older siblings needed phototherapy, or who are not getting enough breast milk are more likely to have severe jaundice. For jaundice lasting more than 14 days in full-term babies, or more than 21 days in premature babies, the NICE guideline calls for a conjugated bilirubin test. Also check whether the stools are pale. If the doctor scheduled a bilirubin recheck at discharge, go at the scheduled time.

### 10. When you start trying for a baby, take 0.4 mg of folic acid daily, continuing until the end of the first 3 months of pregnancy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 准备怀孕就开始每天补 0.4 毫克叶酸，一直吃到孕早期满 3 个月 | f029985d -->
- Cost: Folic acid tablets from a drugstore cost little per month. One tablet a day; the hard part is remembering every day.
- In plain words: Taking one folic acid tablet a day around conception cuts the chance of a neural tube defect in the baby by about 70%. Neural tube defects are birth defects such as anencephaly and spina bifida. For someone who has already had a baby like this, the chance of it happening again falls to about one-third.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review combined 5 trials with 6,708 births, rating the evidence as high quality. Daily folic acid around conception gave a risk of neural tube defects of RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, confidence range), about 70% lower. For people who had already had a baby with a neural tube defect, the risk of recurrence was RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), about one-third of the original. Looking by subgroup, 0.4 mg and higher doses had the same effect. Folic acid alone and multivitamins also had the same effect
- Evidence: A
- Sources: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Notes: The key is "start before you are pregnant". The baby's neural tube closes within 28 days of conception. If you wait until a test shows you are pregnant, most of that time has passed. So if you plan to have a baby, start 3 months in advance. In the same pooled analysis, folic acid had no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease or miscarriage, so do not expect it to prevent other things.

### 11. Throughout pregnancy, not a single cigarette or drink, and family members must not smoke indoors either
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 整个孕期一支烟一口酒都不要，家里人也别在屋里抽 | 2911ece8 -->
- Cost: Free, and it saves money on cigarettes and alcohol. The hard part is getting the people you live with to quit too.
- In plain words: Smoking in pregnancy raises the chance of premature birth, a baby born too small, and cleft lip and palate. It doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and birth. The risk of sudden infant death is also higher. Family members smoking indoors counts too. It is never too late to quit.
- Benefit: The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's position: smoking in pregnancy can cause a baby to be "born too small, even when born full term" and "born too early". It "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery". It raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and cleft palate. It also "damages the developing lungs and brain of the baby, and the damage can last through childhood". Babies whose mothers smoked in pregnancy, and babies exposed to smoke after birth, have a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official advice is "it is best to quit before getting pregnant, but quitting during pregnancy still helps" and "it's never too late to quit"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Notes: Second-hand smoke a pregnant woman breathes when others smoke indoors counts for the baby too. So family members living together should not smoke indoors either. The same goes for alcohol: no amount of drinking is known to be safe, so the simplest course in pregnancy is not to drink at all. For the accounting of smoking and drinking for adults themselves, see Section 20, entry 13 (quit smoking, the earlier the better).

### 12. Put your baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface, in your room but not your bed, with nothing soft in the bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 让孩子仰着睡，睡硬的平面，同房不同床，床上不放任何软东西 | 79096842 -->
- Cost: Free; replace a soft mattress with a firm one. The hard part is standing up to older relatives' views and sticking to this.
- In plain words: Put your baby to sleep face up on a firm, flat surface. Adults and baby sleep in the same room but not the same bed. No pillows, quilts, bumpers, soft toys or head-shaping pillows in the bed. Inclined cradles and baby seats are only for sitting, not for sleep. In the US alone, about 3,500 babies die in their sleep each year.
- Benefit: About 3,500 infants in the US die each year from sleep-related causes. These include sudden infant death syndrome, unknown causes and accidental suffocation in bed. The safe sleep environment from the American Academy of Pediatrics is "supine positioning, use of a firm, noninclined sleep surface, room sharing without bed sharing, and avoidance of soft bedding and overheating". Other things that lower risk: breastfeeding, avoiding exposure to nicotine and alcohol, routine vaccinations and using a pacifier
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Notes: "Nothing soft in the bed" means no pillows, quilts, bumpers, soft toys or head-shaping pillows at all. Not even nursing pillows that look safe. Inclined cradles and baby seats are not places to sleep. If you sleep on futons on tatami, the baby should sleep on its own firm mat and not get under the adults' quilts. Among the newborn entries in this section, this one makes the biggest difference and most needs you to stand up to older relatives.

### 13. However tired or angry you are, never shake a baby
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 无论多累多气都不要摇晃婴儿 | ea038f5a -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is stopping yourself when you are exhausted or furious.
- In plain words: A baby's head is large and their neck is weak. Shaking hard can cause bleeding in their brain and eyes, which can kill them or leave lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to sleep does not count; the danger is uncontrolled violent shaking. When you are about to lose your temper, put the baby back in the cot and walk away for a few minutes; letting them cry for a while is safer than holding and shaking them.
- Benefit: Babies' heads are large in proportion and their neck muscles are weak. Violent shaking causes intracranial and retinal haemorrhage (bleeding in the brain and eyes), which can be fatal or cause lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to sleep does not count; the danger is uncontrolled violent shaking. Medically, this kind of injury from shaking and impact is called "abusive head trauma". It is the leading cause of fatal head injury in children under 2. In the US from 1999 to 2014, it caused nearly 2,250 deaths of children under 5
- Evidence: B
- Sources: Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>；Choudhary AK, et al. (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Notes: When you are truly about to lose control, first put the baby back in the cot and walk away for a few minutes. Letting them cry for a while is much safer than holding and shaking them. People caring for a baby alone and chronically short of sleep most need to remember this. In Japan, without family from back home to help, it is easier to end up carrying it all alone; when you cannot cope, talk to the childcare counter at your ward office.

### 14. Have one gestational diabetes screening at 24 weeks or later; do not skip it because drinking the glucose is a hassle

- Cost: In Japan, the blood glucose test is part of the publicly subsidized prenatal check-ups, so the voucher covers it. Going on an empty stomach takes half a morning.

Japan's prenatal check-up standard tests blood sugar once in early pregnancy and again between 24 and 35 weeks.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force's grade B recommendation: screen pregnant women without symptoms (who do not feel unwell) for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy or later. Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that first appears during pregnancy. If screening only once, it should be at 24 weeks or later. On screening before 24 weeks, the Task Force concluded the current evidence is insufficient to weigh benefits and harms (grade I). Unmanaged gestational diabetes raises the risks of macrosomia (an oversized baby), difficult birth, caesarean section, pre-eclampsia and neonatal hypoglycaemia. MHLW's "desirable standards for health check-ups of pregnant women" set blood glucose tests once in early pregnancy and once between 24 and 35 weeks
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). - Notes: Most of those diagnosed can control their blood sugar through diet and exercise alone; only a minority need insulin. Do not skip this test for fear of being told you need injections. ### 15. Do not give honey to babies under 1

- Cost: Free.
- In plain words: Do not give honey to babies under 1. Honey water, honey mixed into baby food and cakes containing honey all count. Honey may contain bacteria that cause botulism, which ordinary heating and cooking do not kill. Adults' gut bacteria can keep it in check; babies' cannot.
- Benefit: The US CDC says "do not feed honey to children younger than 1 year", because "honey can contain bacteria that can cause botulism". Adults' gut bacteria can suppress these spores (the dormant form of the bacteria); babies' cannot. MHLW also states: honey is a high-risk food for babies under 1; botulinum bacteria resist heat and are not killed by ordinary heating and cooking; do not give babies under 1 honey or drinks and snacks containing honey
- Evidence: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>；厚生労働省「ハチミツを与えるのは１歳を過ぎてから。」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/0000161461.html>
- Notes: Off limits are honey water, baby food with honey mixed in and cakes containing honey. When buying baby snacks, check the ingredients. After the first birthday, honey is no longer a high-risk food.

### 16. Without a medical reason, do not ask for a caesarean section, and do not have one to pick a date
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
<!-- zh: 没有医学指征就别主动要求剖宫产，也别为了挑日子开刀 | 79887c58 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is standing up to what family members say.
- In plain words: According to the World Health Organization, once a place's caesarean rate exceeds 10%, maternal and newborn deaths stop falling further. When a caesarean is needed, it must be done. Having one to pick a date or for fear of pain is just undergoing major abdominal surgery for nothing.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization's 2015 statement: as a population's caesarean rate rises toward about 10%, maternal and newborn deaths fall. Beyond 10%, there is no evidence that mortality improves further. WHO also stresses that every effort should be made to provide caesarean sections to women in need, rather than to achieve any specific rate
- Evidence: A
- Sources: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates (WHO/RHR/15.02). <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Notes: When medically necessary, a caesarean is life-saving surgery; have it, do not tough it out. What to avoid is surgery that is not medically needed, such as for fear of pain, to choose an auspicious date, or to time the child's school entry. A caesarean is major abdominal surgery with slower recovery, and in the next pregnancy the risks of placenta praevia, placenta accreta and uterine rupture are higher. That directly affects whether you want a second child.

### 17. If you have risk factors for pre-eclampsia, take one low-dose aspirin daily from 12 weeks of pregnancy

- Cost: A prescription from your doctor, inexpensive. One tablet a day until delivery. The hard part is not forgetting.
- In plain words: Pre-eclampsia is a high blood pressure disorder of pregnancy. People who had it in a previous pregnancy, are carrying twins or more, have chronic high blood pressure, had diabetes before pregnancy, or have kidney or autoimmune disease are high-risk. Others should not take it on their own.

### 18. If you want an epidural, just ask; it does not increase the risk of caesarean section
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
<!-- zh: 想要无痛分娩就直接说，它不增加剖宫产的风险 | e38df6c4 -->
- Cost: The fee varies by hospital, and not many hospitals offer it, so ask early in your prenatal check-ups. The hard part is having to bring it up yourself.
- In plain words: An epidural (無痛分娩) is an injection of anaesthetic in the lower back that dulls the pain of labour. It works better than other forms of pain relief and does not make a caesarean more likely. The idea that "an epidural gives you back pain for life" also has no basis. In Japan, you can check which hospitals offer it and roughly how much it costs on MHLW's "Shussan Navi" (出産なび) website.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review concluded that epidural analgesia is more effective than non-epidural methods at reducing labour pain and increasing mothers' satisfaction with pain relief. Epidurals for labour use epidural analgesia. The same review says "epidural analgesia had no impact on the risk of caesarean section or long-term backache". The increase in instrumental delivery (delivering the baby with instruments) is no longer seen in studies after 2005. MHLW set up the "Shussan Navi" website, listing for each birth facility the beds, annual number of births, services such as birth partner attendance and epidurals, and average childbirth costs. Information on epidural facilities has since March 2023 been published centrally on the website of the Japanese Association for Labor Analgesia (JALA)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>；厚生労働省 広報誌「厚生労働」2024 年 5 月号「出産なび」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/houdou_kouhou/kouhou_shuppan/magazine/202405_001.html>；厚生労働省「希望に応じた出産を行うための環境整備について」（2025-03-19 資料）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/12401000/001453645.pdf>
- Notes: The two most widespread claims, "an epidural gives you back pain for life" and "an epidural makes labour difficult so you end up with a caesarean", have no basis. Cochrane pooled epidurals against other pain relief and concluded they have no effect on caesarean risk or long-term back pain. The real side effects are: low blood pressure, fever, weak legs, difficulty urinating, and longer first and second stages of labour with a greater chance of needing oxytocin. It is your decision, but do not give it up because of rumours. Ask at prenatal check-ups whether it is available in the middle of the night and whether an anaesthetist is present throughout.

### 19. Breastfeed exclusively for the first 6 months, without even water, and from 6 months add solid foods while continuing to breastfeed

- Cost: Free, and it saves on formula. It takes time: feeding takes up a lot of each day. The hard part is keeping it up without stopping for the first 6 months.
- In plain words: Start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth. For the first 6 months give only breast milk, not even water. From 6 months add solid foods, and keep breastfeeding to 2 years or beyond. If you do not have enough breast milk or cannot breastfeed, use formula and do not blame yourself. What you feed matters far less to the baby's safety than how they sleep.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization recommends "initiating breastfeeding within the first hour of birth". For the first 6 months, "exclusive breastfeeding, meaning no other foods or liquids are provided, including water". Then "introducing safe and adequate complementary foods at 6 months, together with continued breastfeeding up to 2 years of age or beyond"
- Evidence: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>
- Notes: If you do not have enough breast milk, or your health does not allow breastfeeding, use formula.
