# 18. Starting a business: the Business Manager visa, opening notices and social insurance

<!-- zh-intro: e0330198 -->

This section covers what foreigners starting a company or shop in Japan need to work out first: the Business Manager visa's requirements were raised sharply in October 2025, which tax notices a sole proprietor must file, and how social insurance and taxes are checked item by item at visa renewal once you have a company. Information is as of 2026-10-03. For how not to lose everything in the business itself, the general advice in the original book's section on "starting and running a business" is useful.

### 1. A new company with capital of 10 million yen or more pays consumption tax from its first two years: companies set up for the Business Manager visa will usually reach this line
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 资本金 1,000 万日元以上的新公司，头两年也要交消费税：拿经营管理签证的公司资本金多半够这个线 | 819a322d -->
- Cost: Free. Before setting up the company, work out how the amount of capital affects whether you pay consumption tax in the first two years, and build consumption tax returns into your bookkeeping and cash planning.
- In plain words: A new company has no "base period" for its first two years, so in principle it is exempt from consumption tax. But new companies with capital of 10 million yen or more at the start of a period are not exempt, and must file consumption tax from the first year. The Business Manager visa requires capital of 30 million yen or more, so a company set up for this visa will usually owe consumption tax from day one.
- Benefit: The National Tax Agency states: newly established corporations have no base period in their 1st and 2nd periods, so in principle they are exempt from consumption tax; however, corporations whose capital or contributions are 10 million yen or more at the start of that business year (newly established corporations) are not exempt, and if capital at the start of the 1st or 2nd period is 10 million yen or more, they are taxable in that period. Even with capital under 10 million yen, a corporation is not exempt in some cases, such as being a qualified invoice issuer (a business registered for invoices)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国税庁「No.6503 新規開業または法人設立時の納税義務の免除の特例」. <https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6503.htm>；出入国在留管理庁「「経営・管理」の許可基準の改正等について（令和７年１０月１６日施行）」. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001448070.pdf>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. For the Business Manager visa's capital requirement, see entry 3 of this section (capital of 30 million yen). Consumption tax not filed on time will be found at visa renewal; see entry 2 of this section (social insurance and taxes checked item by item). The benefit is rated medium because it avoids back tax and penalties for unfiled consumption tax.

### 2. Once you have a company, you must enrol your employees and yourself in health insurance and the Employees' Pension, and immigration checks social insurance and taxes item by item at visa renewal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 开了公司就要给员工和自己上健康保险、厚生年金，签证更新时入管会逐项查社保和税 | 1f79700c -->
- Cost: Both the company and employees pay social insurance premiums, and the company also pays labour insurance premiums. You must handle paperwork on schedule at the pension office, the Labour Standards Inspection Office and Hello Work.
- In plain words: The Health Insurance Act says any corporate business premises that regularly employs people is a covered workplace for health insurance. The Immigration Services Agency states that renewals of the Business Manager status are checked item by item. Paperwork and payments for employment insurance and workers' accident insurance are checked, enrolment and payment for health insurance and the Employees' Pension are checked, and so are all national and local taxes the company owes. If anything is unpaid, renewal may run into trouble.
- Benefit: The Health Insurance Act provides that "covered workplaces" include business premises of the state, local governments or corporations that regularly employ workers. The Immigration Services Agency's explanation of the revised "Business Manager" permission standards states that at renewal of the period of stay, the fulfilment of the following public charges is confirmed: labour insurance (enrolment of employment insurance insured persons and payment of premiums, and workers' accident insurance coverage procedures); social insurance (enrolment of health insurance and Employees' Pension insured persons and payment of premiums); and national and local taxes owed by the business, namely withholding income tax, corporate tax, consumption tax, corporate resident tax, corporate enterprise tax and so on for corporations, and withholding income tax, self-assessed income tax, consumption tax, individual resident tax, individual enterprise tax and so on for sole proprietors. You must also show the status of any licences required to run the business
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 健康保険法 第三条第三項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/211AC0000000070> ; 出入国在留管理庁「「経営・管理」の許可基準の改正等について（令和７年１０月１６日施行）」. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001448070.pdf>
- Notes: The benefit is rated large because it decides whether the visa can be renewed. In industries that need a business licence, you will also be asked at renewal to prove you hold it. For labour rules on employees such as overtime pay and minimum wage, see Section 7, entry 3 (an hourly wage below the minimum wage is illegal).

### 3. If you want a Business Manager visa by starting a company, check the October 2025 standards first: capital of 30 million yen, 1 full-time employee, and Japanese at about N2 level
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 想靠开公司拿经营管理签证，先对照 2025 年 10 月的新标准：资本金 3,000 万日元、雇 1 名常勤职员、日语 N2 相当 | 8d8e0659 -->
- Cost: Capital of 30 million yen or more. You also need to rent office premises not shared with your home, hire staff, and have a specialist such as a certified SME consultant review your business plan.
- In plain words: From October 16, 2025, the Business Manager status requires capital of 30 million yen or more and at least 1 full-time employee. Only Japanese nationals and people with status-based visas such as Permanent Resident count as employees. You or the employee must have Japanese at about N2 level, and you must have a relevant degree or 3 or more years of business management experience. In principle your own home cannot be used as the office.
- Benefit: The revised "Business Manager" permission standards published by the Immigration Services Agency (in force from October 16, 2025): the company the applicant runs must employ at least 1 full-time employee, limited to Japanese nationals, special permanent residents and foreigners with Appended Table II statuses (Permanent Resident, Spouse or Child of Japanese National, Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident, Long-Term Resident); it must have capital of 30 million yen or more; the applicant or the full-time employee (here including foreigners with Appended Table I statuses) must have a considerable level of Japanese, equivalent to B2 or above on the "Reference Framework for Japanese Language Education", such as JLPT N2 or above, a BJT score of 400 or more, 20 or more years as a mid- to long-term resident in Japan, or graduation from a Japanese university; the applicant must have a doctoral, master's or professional degree in business management or a related field, or 3 or more years of business management experience; and the business plan must be reviewed by a certified SME consultant, certified public accountant, tax accountant or similar. Using your own home as the business premises is in principle not accepted. People already holding Business Manager status who apply for renewal within 3 years of the change taking effect (by October 16, 2028) will be judged with reference to their business situation and prospects of meeting the standards, even if they do not meet the new standards
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国在留管理庁「「経営・管理」の許可基準の改正等について（令和７年１０月１６日施行）」（published 2025-10-10, updated 10-30）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001448070.pdf>
- Notes: The benefit is rated large because it decides whether you can get the visa. After the change, people who do not meet the new standards cannot apply for permanent residence from Business Manager status. Long absences abroad during the period of stay without good reason will lead to renewal being refused. The Immigration Services Agency warns that people other than lawyers and administrative scriveners who charge to prepare application documents may be violating the Administrative Scrivener Act, so check credentials when using an agent.

### 4. Within 2 months of registering a company, file a notice of incorporation with the tax office; to use blue-form returns, apply within 3 months, and losses can then be carried forward 10 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 公司登记后 2 个月内向税务署交法人设立届出，想用蓝色申告的 3 个月内申请，亏损能往后抵 10 年 | a4f1ce0e -->
- Cost: Free. File the notice of incorporation with a copy of the articles of incorporation, through e-Tax or on paper. Blue-form filing needs a separate application for approval, and after that you must keep books as required.
- In plain words: After setting up a company in Japan, you must file a notice of incorporation with the tax office within 2 months of the registration date. To use blue-form returns (青色申告) from the first year, apply before the earlier of two dates: 3 months after incorporation, or the end of the first business year. With blue-form returns, a loss in any year can be set against profits over the following 10 years.
- Benefit: The National Tax Agency states: a domestic ordinary corporation must submit a "notice of incorporation" with a copy of its articles of incorporation and the like to the district tax office within 2 months from the date of incorporation (the date of registration). To have blue-form approval from the first period, the application deadline is the day before the earlier of the date 3 months after incorporation and the end of the first business year. The agency also states that losses arising in business years for which blue-form returns were filed can be deducted in calculating income for business years beginning within 10 years before each later business year (9 years for business years beginning before April 1, 2018)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国税庁「No.5100 新設法人の届出書類」. <https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/hojin/5100.htm>；国税庁「No.5762 青色申告書を提出した事業年度の欠損金の繰越控除」（令和 7 年 4 月 1 日現在法令等）. <https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/hojin/5762.htm>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. Companies usually make losses in their first few years, and if you miss the blue-form deadline, those losses cannot be carried forward. The benefit is rated medium because the tax offset by losses is commonly tens of thousands of yen or more. For a sole proprietor's tax filings, see entry 5 of this section (file an opening notice for a sole proprietorship).

### 5. If you open a business as a sole proprietor, file an opening notice with the tax office, and to save tax apply for blue-form returns in time, with a deduction of up to 650,000 yen
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 个人开店，向税务署交开业申报，想省税就在期限内申请蓝色申报，最多扣 65 万日元 | 972fe198 -->
- Cost: Free. File two forms: the notice of opening or closing a sole proprietorship, and the application for approval of blue-form income tax returns. After that, keep books as required.
- In plain words: When you start a business as an individual, you must file an opening notice with the tax office by the final return deadline for the year you opened. To use blue-form returns, apply in principle by March 15 of that year. If you open on or after January 16, apply within 2 months of opening. Blue-form filers can deduct 550,000 yen from income each year, or 650,000 yen if they keep books electronically or file through e-Tax.
- Benefit: The National Tax Agency states: the "notice of opening or closing a sole proprietorship" when newly starting a business is due by the final return deadline for the year in which the business started. The "application for approval of blue-form income tax returns" is in principle due by March 15 of the year you want to start filing blue-form returns; for businesses newly started on or after January 16 of that year, within 2 months of starting. Blue-form filers can deduct a special blue-form deduction of 550,000 yen (650,000 yen if certain requirements are met) or 100,000 yen from their income; the 650,000 yen requirement is, in addition to the 550,000 yen requirements, keeping the journal and general ledger as electronic books, or filing the return and financial statements through e-Tax by the final return deadline. The 550,000 and 650,000 yen deductions only apply if the return is filed by March 15 of the following year
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国税庁「No.2090 新たに事業を始めたときの届出など」（令和８年４月１日現在法令等）. <https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2090.htm> ; 国税庁「No.2072 青色申告特別控除」（令和８年４月１日現在法令等）. <https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2072.htm>
- Notes: The deduction comes off income, so the tax saved depends on your rate; at common rates it is tens of thousands to over 100,000 yen a year, so the benefit is rated medium. If your status of residence does not allow business activity (for example, Student or Dependent without permission), you cannot open a business this way; see Section 2, entry 10 (get permission for activities outside your status before working part-time).

### 6. Companies with fewer than 10 employees can apply for the "special payment schedule" for withholding tax, paying withheld income tax just twice a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
<!-- zh: 员工不到 10 个人的公司，申请源泉税「纳期特例」，代扣的所得税一年交两次就行 | 58b6d808 -->
- Cost: Free. Submit one "application for approval of the special payment schedule for withholding income tax" to the tax office.
- In plain words: Income tax a company withholds from salaries must in principle be paid by the 10th of the month after salaries are paid. If fewer than 10 people are regularly paid salaries, you can apply for the special payment schedule (納期の特例). Tax withheld from January to June is paid by July 10, and tax withheld from July to December by January 20 of the next year. Just two trips a year.
- Benefit: The National Tax Agency states: withheld income tax and special income tax for reconstruction must in principle be paid by the 10th of the month after salaries and the like are actually paid; withholding agents who regularly pay salaries to fewer than 10 people can pay half a year's worth together, called the special payment schedule. It applies only to tax withheld from salaries, retirement allowances, and certain fees such as those of tax accountants, lawyers and judicial scriveners. Once it applies, tax withheld from January to June is paid by July 10, and tax withheld from July to December by January 20 of the following year. An application for approval must be submitted; if the tax office does not notify a rejection, it is deemed approved at the end of the month after submission
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国税庁「No.2505 源泉所得税及び復興特別所得税の納付期限と納期の特例」. <https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/gensen/2505.htm>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. If the number of people regularly paid rises to 10 or more, you must file a notice that you no longer qualify. Payment of withholding income tax is also checked at visa renewal; see entry 2 of this section (social insurance and taxes checked item by item). The benefit is rated small because it saves ten trips to the tax office a year.
