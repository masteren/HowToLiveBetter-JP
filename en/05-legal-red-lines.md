# 5. Japan's legal red lines: don't let one "part-time job" wreck your residence

<!-- zh-intro: 60b4b085 -->

This section covers the crimes foreigners in Japan are most easily pulled into: illegal jobs recruited online, selling bank accounts and phone numbers, lending out your residence card, introducing others to illegal work, cannabis, and what to do if you are arrested. For foreigners the consequences often come in two layers: a criminal penalty in Japan, and revocation of residence status or deportation. The statutes have been checked against the versions on e-Gov in force in 2026-10.

### 1. Do not lend, buy or sell a residence card, and do not use someone else's
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 在留卡不借、不买、不卖，也不用别人的 | 92f2caee -->
- Cost: Free. If a friend asks to borrow your card to apply for a job or open a bank account, just say no.
- In plain words: Lending your residence card for someone else to use, or using another person's card, carries up to 1 year. These acts are in themselves grounds for deportation, without a conviction first, and helping or encouraging someone to do them also counts. Using a fake residence card, or buying or selling one, carries at least 1 year and up to 10 years.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, providing a residence card in your own name for someone to use, or using, providing, receiving or possessing a card in another person's name, is punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen. Forging or altering a residence card, or using, providing or receiving a forged or altered card, is punishable by imprisonment of 1 to 10 years. Possessing a forged card is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years or a fine of up to 500,000 yen. Anyone who commits, incites or aids these acts, including providing a card in your own name for use and providing, receiving or possessing a card in another's name, falls under the grounds for deportation, and this provision does not require a criminal sentence
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第七十三条の三第一項・第二項・第三項、第七十三条の四第一項、第七十三条の六第一項、第二十四条第三号の五. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: If you lose your card, get a replacement within 14 days instead of making do with someone else's; see Section 2, entry 7 (if you lose your residence card). When a company or landlord asks to see your residence card, showing the original or giving a copy is fine. Handing it over for them to keep, or lending it to someone to use, crosses the line.

### 2. Do not introduce friends to illegal work; if you run a business that hires foreigners, check their residence card for work permission first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 不介绍朋友去打黑工；自己开店雇外国人，先看在留卡上能不能工作 | b94053d3 -->
- Cost: Free. Before hiring, check the "work restrictions" field on the residence card and any permission for activities outside status. Then use immigration's residence card reader app or the card invalidation lookup to confirm the card is genuine and valid.
- In plain words: Having a foreigner without work permission do work, or making a business of introducing such work, carries up to 3 years and a 3 million yen fine. Saying "I didn't know they couldn't work" does not get you off unless you were truly not negligent. A foreigner who does any of this falls under the grounds for deportation by that act alone.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, having a foreigner engage in illegal work in the course of business, placing a foreigner under your control so that they work illegally, or making a business of introducing such work, is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 3 million yen, or both. Illegal work means paid activity beyond the status of residence, part-time work without permission for activities outside status, work by people overstaying, and the like. People who commit these acts cannot escape punishment on the ground that they did not know the foreigner's activity exceeded their status, that the foreigner lacked permission, or that the foreigner was overstaying, unless they were not negligent. Anyone who commits, incites or aids these acts falls under the grounds for deportation
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第七十三条の二第一項・第二項、第二十四条第三号の四. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 出入国在留管理庁「在留カードとは？」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/whatzairyu_00001.html>
- Notes: The Immigration Services Agency states that the new residence cards issued from June 14, 2026 no longer print several items, such as the period of stay, on the card face. You need the reader app version of August 19, 2026 or later to see them. Students and Dependent visa holders have a 28-hour weekly limit on part-time work; see Section 2, entry 10 (no more than 28 hours a week). Charging people from your home country a fee for "introducing jobs" is the easiest way to fall under the "making a business of introducing" clause.

### 3. Cannabis is punished as a narcotic in Japan: do not use it, carry it, or hold it for anyone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 大麻在日本按麻药处罚，不吸、不带、不替人保管 | e40644c9 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain words: Japan's narcotics law treats cannabis as a narcotic. Using, possessing or handing it over carries up to 7 years; doing it for profit carries 1 to 10 years, with a possible extra fine of 3 million yen. It makes no difference that you bought it legally abroad; possessing it in Japan is illegal. A foreigner sentenced for a drug offence falls under the grounds for deportation, whatever the length of the sentence.
- Benefit: The Narcotics and Psychotropics Control Act states that the narcotics it covers include cannabis. Using narcotics without being a licensed narcotics user is punishable by imprisonment of up to 7 years; for profit, by imprisonment of 1 to 10 years, with a possible fine of up to 3 million yen in addition. Unauthorized preparation, subdivision, transfer, receipt or possession of narcotics (other than diacetylmorphine and some others) is punishable by imprisonment of up to 7 years; for profit, by imprisonment of 1 to 10 years, with a possible fine of up to 3 million yen in addition. Attempts are also punished. Under the Immigration Act, a foreigner sentenced for violating narcotics-related laws falls under the grounds for deportation, with no requirement on the length of the sentence
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 麻薬及び向精神薬取締法（version in force from 2026-10-01）第二条第一号、第二十七条、第六十六条、第六十六条の二. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/328AC0000000014> ; 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十四条第四号チ. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: Bringing it in from abroad, or having a friend forward it to you, falls under the same law in Japan. If a friend asks you to "just hold a package" that turns out to contain cannabis, it is hard to explain if found, so do not accept packages of unknown origin for others.

### 4. Do not carry a knife when you go out: carrying a blade over 6 cm without a legitimate reason is illegal, with up to 2 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 不随身带刀出门：刃长超过 6 厘米的刀没有正当理由带着就违法，最重判 2 年 | 1230f8dd -->
- Cost: Free. Keep knives at home. If you take one out for moving, camping or cooking, wrap it, put it in a bag, and bring it straight back after use.
- In plain words: In Japan, going out with a knife whose blade is longer than 6 cm, without work or another legitimate reason, is illegal. The maximum is imprisonment of up to 2 years or a fine of up to 300,000 yen. Scissors and folding knives of 8 cm or less are exceptions. Concealing a shorter knife without a legitimate reason can also be punished under the Minor Offenses Act. "Self-defence" is not a legitimate reason.
- Benefit: Article 22 of the Firearms and Swords Control Act provides that no one may carry a knife with a blade longer than 6 cm, measured as set out by Cabinet Office ordinance, except for work or another legitimate reason; scissors and folding knives with a blade of 8 cm or less, and other knives of types or shapes set by cabinet order, are excluded. Article 31-18, paragraph 2 of that Act provides that violating Article 22 of that Act is punishable by imprisonment of up to 2 years or a fine of up to 300,000 yen. Article 1, item 2 of the Minor Offenses Act provides that concealing and carrying, without a legitimate reason, a knife, iron bar or other implement that could be used to take life or seriously injure the body is punishable by penal detention (拘留) or a petty fine (科料)
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 銃砲刀剣類所持等取締法 第 22 条、第 31 条の 18. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/333AC0000000006>；軽犯罪法 第 1 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/323AC0000000039>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. "Self-defence is not a legitimate reason" is this book's judgment; the statute only says "work or another legitimate reason", so if in doubt, do not carry it. For what an imprisonment sentence means for your residence, see Section 2, entry 5 (a sentence can get you deported). The benefit is rated large because it avoids criminal liability.

### 5. Never touch online jobs promising "high pay, cash the same day, legit work"; if you are already hooked, call #9110 or go to a police station
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 网上「高收入、当天结、白活儿」的打工一律不碰，已经上了钩就打 #9110 或者去警察署 | 1c9a9ed8 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting when you see "50,000 yen a day", and daring to go to the police once you are hooked.
- In plain words: Job ads on social media that do not say what the work is, only that it pays a lot, are mostly recruiting people for fraud or robbery; in Japan these are called "yami baito" (闇バイト). If you are moved to chat apps such as Signal or Telegram, the danger is greater. Doing it under threat is still a crime. If you have already applied, go to the police at once; the police say they will protect you and your family.
- Benefit: The National Police Agency states that on social media and online boards there are posts recruiting people to carry out crimes, which do not explain the work and hint at unusually high pay, and many people who applied took part in robbery or fraud and were arrested. Be wary of ads stressing "easy, simple, high income", such as "high pay", "cash the same day" or "legit work" (ホワイト案件). If you are steered to highly anonymous apps such as Signal or Telegram, the risk of being drawn into crime is high. Even if you act because you or your family are threatened, robbery is a serious violent crime. The agency urges anyone who has already applied to go to the police at once, and says the police will protect those who come forward and their families. You can call the police consultation line #9110 or go to a nearby police station. Criminal groups never intend to pay the promised money; applicants are "used and thrown away". Under the Penal Code, fraud is punishable by imprisonment of up to 10 years, and robbery by imprisonment for a fixed term of 5 years or more
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 警察庁「いわゆる「闇バイト」の危険性について」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.npa.go.jp/bureau/safetylife/yamibaito/hanzaishaboshu.html> ; 警察庁生活安全企画課 (2024). ＳＮＳなどで求人情報を探している方へ 犯罪実行者募集情報の特徴. <https://www.npa.go.jp/bureau/safetylife/yamibaito/tokutyou.pdf> ; 刑法 第二百三十六条、第二百四十六条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/140AC0000000045>
- Notes: Among the recruiting phrases listed by the National Police Agency, common ones are "carrying jobs", "driver", "pick-up and drop-off" and "delivering documents", and some require a driver's licence. They lead you to an anonymous app to send personal information, then use that information to threaten you, so never send photos of your ID or your address. Work-visa holders and students sentenced to imprisonment for fraud or robbery can be deported even with a suspended sentence; see Section 2, entry 5 (do not commit crimes such as theft, fraud or assault).

### 6. Before leaving Japan, do not sell your bank account or phone number; close the account at the bank and cancel the phone contract with the carrier
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 离开日本前别卖银行账户和手机号，账户去银行注销，手机去运营商解约 | f162e584 -->
- Cost: Free. Before leaving Japan, make one trip each to the bank and the phone shop, an hour or two each.
- In plain words: Transferring your bankbook, bank card or online banking passwords to someone for payment, without a legitimate reason, carries up to 3 years and a 5 million yen fine. To transfer a phone contracted in your name to anyone other than family, you need the carrier's consent first. Selling these is supplying fraud groups with tools to receive money and make calls. Close and cancel them before you leave.
- Benefit: The Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds provides that transferring, delivering or providing to another person for payment a bankbook, cash card, or information needed for withdrawals or transfers, without a legitimate reason such as ordinary business or financial transactions, is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 5 million yen, or both. Giving them to someone knowing they will use them while posing as another person carries the same penalty. Doing it as a business is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years or a fine of up to 10 million yen, or both. Soliciting or advertising for people to do this carries the same penalty as the former. The Act on Prevention of Improper Use of Mobile Phones provides that a subscriber who transfers a phone or SIM card in their name to anyone other than relatives or people living with them must first obtain the carrier's consent. Transferring them for payment as a business for profit is punishable by imprisonment of up to 2 years or a fine of up to 3 million yen, or both
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 犯罪による収益の移転防止に関する法律（version in force from 2026-08-12）第二十六条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/419AC0000000022> ; 携帯音声通信事業者による契約者等の本人確認等及び携帯音声通信役務の不正な利用の防止に関する法律 第七条第一項、第二十条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/417AC1000000031>
- Notes: "I won't need it back home anyway, so I might as well sell it" is exactly the typical line in these cases. If money will still come into the account after you leave, such as your last month's salary or a tax refund, agree on how to receive it with your employer and tax agent first, then close the account. The pension lump-sum withdrawal payment can be sent to an account in your country of origin; see Section 4, entry 3 (the pension lump-sum withdrawal payment).

### 7. Do not play at online casinos that are "legal overseas": connecting from Japan to gamble is the crime of gambling, free versions and bonus points included
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 不在网上玩「海外合法」的网络赌场：在日本国内连上去赌就是赌博罪，免费版和送的积分也不例外 | 7e4ce804 -->
- Cost: Free. Do not register or deposit money, and delete apps and groups that promote online casinos.
- In plain words: The National Police Agency states that even if an online casino operates legally overseas, gambling on it from inside Japan is a crime. Baccarat, slots and sports betting are all the same, and stay away from free versions and bonus points too. Gambling is punishable by a fine of up to 500,000 yen, and habitual gambling by up to 3 years. The police have already arrested people who gambled on a computer at home.
- Benefit: The National Police Agency states that gambling from Japan on an online casino legally operated overseas is also a crime; whether paid or a "free version" or "free bonus (points)", you must stop using it completely; the same applies whatever the name or content, including baccarat, slots and sports betting. Enforcement cases listed by the agency include a gambler who connected from a home computer in Japan to an online casino run by an overseas company, bet against the site's dealer, and was charged with gambling. Article 185 of the Penal Code provides that gambling is punishable by a fine of up to 500,000 yen or a petty fine, except when betting only things for momentary entertainment; Article 186 of the Penal Code provides that habitual gambling is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 警察庁「オンラインカジノを利用した賭博は犯罪です！」（accessed 2026-10-04）. <https://www.npa.go.jp/bureau/safetylife/hoan/onlinecasino/onlinecasino.html>；刑法 第 185 条、第 186 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/140AC0000000045>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-04. Being legal in your country of origin or elsewhere does not make it legal in Japan; what counts is where you are when you gamble. A fine still gives you a criminal record. For what imprisonment means for your residence, see Section 2, entry 5 (a sentence can get you deported). If you can no longer control it and are borrowing to gamble, first contact a public health center or mental health and welfare center; see Section 1, entry 13 (gambling to the point of borrowing to repay). ### 8. Do not download paid manga, books, films or music from pirate sites: knowingly downloading pirated works repeatedly carries up to 2 years, and for music and video knowingly downloading even once counts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 不从盗版网站下载正版要收钱的漫画、书、影视和音乐：明知是盗版还反复下载，最重判 2 年，音乐和影像明知是盗版下载一次就算 | f1678e5e -->
- Cost: You pay for legitimate copies, or use libraries and legitimate free platforms. The difficulty is that pirated copies are one click away, while legitimate ones mean waiting and paying.
- In plain words: Japan's Copyright Act covers downloading, for your own use, works that are sold commercially. Recording music or video while knowing it is pirated carries up to 2 years of imprisonment or a fine of up to 2 million yen, or both. For other works it only counts if done repeatedly, and downloads of only a small part or at very low quality do not count.
- Benefit: Article 119, paragraph 3 of the Copyright Act provides for imprisonment of up to 2 years or a fine of up to 2 million yen, or both, for: anyone who, for private use, knowing that an automatic public transmission infringes rights, receives it and makes sound or video recordings of commercially offered recordings or videos, infringing copyright or neighbouring rights; and anyone who, for private use, knowing that an automatic public transmission infringes rights, receives it and digitally copies commercially offered works (other than sound and video recordings), continuously or repeatedly. The latter excludes copying that is minor in view of the proportion copied, image resolution and the like, and special cases where, given the type of work and manner of copying, the rights holder's interests are not unreasonably harmed. Paragraph 5 of the same article provides that not knowing a copy was pirated through gross negligence does not count. Article 123 of the Copyright Act provides that these offences can only be prosecuted on a complaint from the rights holder
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 著作権法 第 30 条、第 119 条、第 123 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/345AC0000000048>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-04. The criminal penalty requires "knowing", and for other works also "repeatedly", but in civil law the rights holder can still claim damages; that is a separate matter. Uploading or sharing what you downloaded is a separate and more serious offence, so do not. For what imprisonment means for your residence, see Section 2, entry 5 (a sentence can get you deported). The benefit is rated large because it avoids criminal liability; actual prosecution requires a complaint from the rights holder.

### 9. If you are arrested, first say "当番弁護士を呼んでください" (please call the duty lawyer) to see a lawyer once for free, then ask for your consulate to be notified
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 被逮捕了先说「当番弁護士を呼んでください」，免费见一次律师，再要求通知本国领事馆 | 8576ab85 -->
- Cost: The first visit from a duty lawyer is free. Hiring a lawyer formally after that costs extra. If you are detained and cannot afford a lawyer, you can apply for a court-appointed lawyer paid by the state.
- In plain words: Within 72 hours of arrest, a decision must be made to indict you or to request detention. Detention is first 10 days, and with a judge's approval can be extended by up to 10 more; if you are not indicted by then, you must be released. You can call a lawyer once for free by saying "当番弁護士を呼んでください", and your family can also call one for you. Police are not present when the lawyer meets you. Foreigners can also ask for their consulate to be notified.
- Benefit: The Code of Criminal Procedure provides that after an arrest the police must tell the suspect the gist of the alleged facts and that they may appoint defence counsel, and that they may apply to a designated bar association to have counsel appointed. No more than 72 hours may pass from the start of custody to the decision to indict or request detention. If no indictment is made within 10 days of the detention request, the suspect must be released; when the judge finds unavoidable reasons, the period may be extended by no more than 10 days in total. The Japan Federation of Bar Associations states that an arrested person can call a lawyer once for a free consultation under the duty lawyer system (当番弁護士制度); the person can say to the police, prosecutor or judge "please call the duty lawyer", and family members can also call on their behalf; the lawyer can meet the person even when family and friends cannot visit, and no police officer is present. The Vienna Convention on Consular Relations provides that when a foreigner is arrested or detained, the local authorities must, if the person so requests, notify their consulate without delay, and must inform the person of this right without delay
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 刑事訴訟法（version in force from 2026-08-13）第二百三条、第二百五条第二項、第二百八条、第二百八条の二. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/323AC0000000131> ; 日本弁護士連合会「逮捕されたとき」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.nichibenren.or.jp/legal_advice/gaiyou/arrest.html> ; 日本弁護士連合会「刑事弁護に関する制度のご紹介」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.nichibenren.or.jp/activity/criminal/keijibengo/seido.html> ; United Nations (1963). Vienna Convention on Consular Relations, Article 36. <https://legal.un.org/ilc/texts/instruments/english/conventions/9_2_1963.pdf>
- Notes: The 23 days apply to ordinary cases; for a few offences such as insurrection and riot, the judge can extend further. The benefit is rated medium on the basis that "acting correctly during those days of custody limits the damage"; no study gives a figure. The Japan Federation of Bar Associations states that the suspect does not pay for the first visit, nor for the interpreter at a foreigner's first visit. For what a sentence means for your residence, see Section 2, entry 5 (a sentence can get you deported).
