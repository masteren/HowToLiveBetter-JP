# 8. Renting and moving: deposits, agent fees and guarantors

<!-- zh-intro: bcfce88b -->

This section covers where renting in Japan most easily costs you extra: too much deducted from your deposit when you move out, excessive agent fees, acting as someone's guarantor, and who to turn to when you cannot rent because you are a foreigner. The Civil Code provisions have been checked on e-Gov, and the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) materials are as of 2026-10-03.

### 1. Do not readily act as a friend's rental guarantor; if you do, the contract must state a maximum amount, or the guarantee is void
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 别轻易替朋友当租房保证人；要当，合同里必须写明最高额，没写的无效 | 1737ba5d -->
- Cost: Free. When someone asks you to be their guarantor, first look at the maximum guarantee amount in the contract, and think about whether you could afford to pay it.
- In plain words: As a rental guarantor, you can be asked to pay the other person's unpaid rent, damages for harm to the property, and penalties. When an individual gives this kind of guarantee, the contract must state "the most you will pay"; without it the guarantee is void, and the guarantee contract must also be in writing. Many properties now use guarantee companies; if you can use one, do not ask a friend.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that under a revolving guarantee contract by an individual (not a corporation), where the principal obligation is unspecified debts within a certain range, the guarantor is liable for the principal, interest, penalties, damages and all other accessory obligations, as well as any agreed penalty or damages on the guarantee obligation, up to the total "maximum amount" (極度額). An individual revolving guarantee contract that does not set a maximum amount has no effect. A guarantee contract not made in writing has no effect
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 民法 第四百四十六条第二項、第四百六十五条の二. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/129AC0000000089>
- Notes: The beneficiary is mainly in tier ③: when friends or people from your hometown ask you to be their guarantor, you are the one taking the risk. If they return to their country of origin and cannot be reached, the landlord can only come after you. The benefit is rated large because maximum amounts are often written at several hundred thousand yen or more.

### 2. When you move out, pay only for damage you caused: ordinary wear and ageing should not cost you, so take photos on the day you move in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 退房时只赔自己弄坏的：日常使用的磨损和老化不该让你出钱，入住当天先拍照 | 9e9f2b9e -->
- Cost: Free. On the day you move in, photograph or film every room, wall, floor and fixture, take close-ups of any scratches or stains, and keep them.
- In plain words: The Civil Code states that tenants are not responsible for restoring wear from normal living or the property simply getting older. Pin holes, dark marks on the wall behind the fridge, sun fading and dents from furniture should all be paid by the landlord. Only what you damaged yourself, yellowing from smoking, and grease and mould built up from not cleaning are yours to pay. Take photos on move-in day so you can make your case when you move out.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that a tenant must restore damage to the property that arose after moving in when the lease ends; but this does not include wear from ordinary use and ageing, nor damage not attributable to the tenant. MLIT's "Guidelines on Restoration Disputes" list the following as the landlord's cost: floor dents and marks from furniture; dark marks on the wall behind the TV and fridge; marks from posters on walls; pin and thumbtack holes that do not need the backing board replaced; wallpaper discoloured by sunlight; professional whole-unit cleaning, provided the tenant cleaned normally; changing locks when keys were not damaged or lost; and breakdown of equipment at the end of its life. Listed as the tenant's cost: tar and smell from smoking; scratches made while moving; kitchen grease from not cleaning; mould from condensation left to spread; and nail or screw holes that need the backing board replaced. The guidelines also require the tenant's share to be reduced according to the age of the item; for example, carpet has a residual value of 1 yen after 6 years, so the longer it has been used, the less the tenant pays
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 民法 第六百二十一条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/129AC0000000089> ; 国土交通省住宅局 (2011). 原状回復をめぐるトラブルとガイドライン（再改訂版）. <https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001016469.pdf>
- Notes: The benefit is rated medium because typical over-deductions are tens of thousands of yen. The contract may contain "special clauses" (特約) making the tenant pay for cleaning, replacing tatami and the like. The guidelines state that such clauses can only be valid if they are necessary and reasonable, and the tenant clearly understood and explicitly agreed to them. Read the special clauses carefully before signing.

### 3. Agent fees for both sides together are capped at one month's rent; charging your side more than half a month needs your prior consent
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 中介费两边加起来最多一个月房租，向你一方收超过半个月的，要你事先同意 | f8b24510 -->
- Cost: Free. Before signing, ask how much the agent fee is, and check whether the application or contract contains a line saying "I agree to pay one month's agent fee".
- In plain words: For rentals, the agent fee from landlord and tenant together is at most one month's rent, plus consumption tax. For residential rentals, without your prior consent the agent can charge the tenant's side at most half a month. Many agents have you sign a line of consent when you apply, so think before signing.
- Benefit: The MLIT notice on remuneration for real estate brokers provides: when brokering a lease, the total remuneration received from both clients may not exceed one month's rent (excluding consumption tax). When brokering a lease of a residential building, the remuneration from one client may not exceed half a month's rent, except where that client consented when the brokerage was requested
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 国土交通省「宅地建物取引業者が宅地又は建物の売買等に関して受けることができる報酬の額」（昭和 45 年建設省告示第 1552 号）. <https://www.mlit.go.jp/notice/noticedata/sgml/1970/26197000/26197000.html>
- Notes: The benefit is rated medium because half a month's rent is often tens of thousands of yen. For miscellaneous fees on the quote that you do not understand, ask the agent to explain each one and whether it can be dropped.

### 4. Before signing a lease, check whether it is a "fixed-term lease": the landlord can refuse to renew at the end, so if you want to stay long, do not sign one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 签租房合同先看是不是「定期借家」：到期房东可以不续，想长住就别签 | 547206c9 -->
- Cost: Free. Before signing, check the contract title and the explanation of important matters for "定期建物賃貸借" or "定期借家", and ask whether you can sign again when it ends.
- In plain words: Under an ordinary lease, the landlord cannot refuse renewal at the end without a legitimate reason. A fixed-term lease (定期借家) is different: it ends at expiry, and if the landlord does not want to renew, you have to move. The landlord must give you a separate written explanation before signing; without it, the "no renewal" clause is void. For terms of 1 year or more, the landlord must notify you between 1 year and 6 months before expiry.
- Benefit: Article 28 of the Act on Land and Building Leases provides that a landlord may refuse renewal or give notice of termination only if a legitimate reason is found, considering both parties' need to use the building, the history, the state of use, and any relocation payment the landlord offers. Article 38 of that Act provides: for a building lease with a fixed term, a no-renewal clause can only be agreed if the contract is in writing, such as a notarial deed. The lessor must give the lessee in advance a document stating that "the lease will not be renewed and ends at expiry", and explain it; without the explanation, the no-renewal clause is void. For terms of 1 year or more, the lessor must notify the lessee between 1 year and 6 months before expiry that the lease ends at expiry, or cannot assert the termination against the lessee; if notice is given after that window, termination takes effect 6 months after the notice. For residential premises under 200 square metres, a lessee who finds it hard to keep living there for unavoidable reasons such as a job transfer, medical treatment or caring for a relative may give notice of termination
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 借地借家法 第 28 条、第 38 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/403AC0000000090>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. Rent on fixed-term leases is sometimes cheaper than on ordinary ones, which suits a short stay; if you plan to stay several years or your children will go to school nearby, do the sums. If the landlord wants you out at expiry and you cannot agree, you can first use Houterasu's free legal consultation; see Section 9, entry 5 (Houterasu). The benefit is rated medium because it avoids the moving costs and new key money and agent fees of a forced move.

### 5. Get an itemized statement for your deposit refund: after deducting unpaid rent and what you owe for damage, the rest must be returned
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 押金退还要拿到明细：押金扣掉欠的房租和该你赔的部分，剩下的都要退 | b18db58c -->
- Cost: Free. After moving out, ask the landlord or management company for an itemized statement of deductions, and check each item against your move-in photos and the MLIT guidelines.
- In plain words: The Civil Code states that money given to secure rent and similar debts is a deposit, whatever it is called. After the lease ends and the property is handed back, the landlord must return the deposit minus what you owe. Once you have the statement, cross out the items that are normal wear according to the guidelines, then negotiate.
- Benefit: The Civil Code provides that a security deposit (敷金) means money, whatever it is called, paid by the tenant to the landlord to secure rent and other monetary debts the tenant owes the landlord under the lease. When the lease ends and the property is handed back, or when the tenant lawfully assigns the lease, the landlord must return to the tenant the balance of the deposit after deducting those debts. If the tenant fails to pay a monetary debt, the landlord may apply the deposit to it; the tenant may not demand that the landlord use the deposit to pay rent. Who pays for restoration is judged under Article 621 of the Civil Code and the MLIT guidelines
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 民法 第六百二十二条の二. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/129AC0000000089> ; 裁判所「少額訴訟」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.courts.go.jp/saiban/syurui/syurui_minzi/minzi_25_02/index.html> ; 消費者庁「消費者ホットライン」. <https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/local_cooperation/local_consumer_administration/hotline/> ; 国土交通省住宅局 (2011). 原状回復をめぐるトラブルとガイドライン（再改訂版）. <https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001016469.pdf>
- Notes: If you cannot agree, call the consumer hotline 188, which connects you to a nearby consumer affairs center. In court, claims of 600,000 yen or less can use small claims proceedings (少額訴訟). In principle there is one hearing, and within 2 weeks of the judgment you can only file an objection, not an appeal. Each person can use it at most 10 times a year at the same court, and as a rule you pay your own lawyer's fees. If you are returning to your country of origin after moving out, settle it before you leave; chasing it after you have gone is much harder.

### 6. If you cannot rent because you are a foreigner, use MLIT's safety-net housing search, or contact a local housing support corporation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
<!-- zh: 因为是外国人租不到房，用国交省的安全网住宅检索，或者找当地的居住支援法人 | 67dc60bb -->
- Cost: Free. Search the "safety-net housing information system", or ask your municipality whether it has a housing support council or housing support corporations.
- In plain words: Japan has registered rental properties that undertake not to refuse certain groups of people, called safety-net housing (セーフティネット住宅), and MLIT has a dedicated website to search them. An MLIT ordinance lists foreigners as "people requiring housing assistance". Many areas also have housing support corporations (居住支援法人) that help these people find homes and talk to landlords.
- Benefit: MLIT states that the housing safety-net system consists of rental housing registered as not refusing people requiring housing assistance (safety-net housing) and other elements. The law defines people requiring housing assistance as low-income people, disaster victims, elderly people, people with disabilities and households raising children; the ordinance adds foreigners, Japanese left behind in China (中国残留邦人), abused children, victims of domestic violence and others. Local governments can add more in their plans. Registered properties are listed on the "safety-net housing information system" website. Housing support councils bring together local governments, related businesses and housing support groups to provide housing information and other support to both people requiring assistance and landlords
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 国土交通省「住宅セーフティネット制度」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.mlit.go.jp/jutakukentiku/house/jutakukentiku_house_tk3_000055.html> ; 国土交通省「住宅セーフティネット制度Ｑ＆Ａ」. <https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001207615.pdf> ; 国土交通省「セーフティネット住宅情報提供システム」. <https://safetynet-jutaku.mlit.go.jp/guest/>
- Notes: Why grade B: the system is stated officially, but there is no figure for how quickly it finds you a home.
