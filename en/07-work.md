# 7. Work: wages, overtime pay, dismissal and workplace injury

<!-- zh-intro: 90bdb320 -->

This section covers the things people most often lose out on in part-time and full-time work in Japan. Japan's labour law and social insurance apply regardless of nationality, so foreigners are covered exactly like Japanese workers, including students working part-time. The statutes have been checked against the 2026 versions on e-Gov, and amounts are as of 2026-10-03.

### 1. Hours beyond 8 a day or 40 a week must be paid as overtime; keep a record of when you start and finish work
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 每天超过 8 小时、每周超过 40 小时的部分要给加班费，记下自己的上下班时间 | 4c04b5b5 -->
- Cost: Free. Note your start and finish times every day, photograph rosters and time-card records, and keep your pay slips.
- In plain words: Work beyond 8 hours a day or 40 hours a week is overtime, paid at least 25% extra. Work on a statutory day off is paid 35% extra, and work from 10 p.m. to 5 a.m. 25% extra. Overtime beyond 60 hours in a month is paid at least 50% extra. Unpaid overtime can be claimed for up to 3 years.
- Benefit: The Labor Standards Act provides that, excluding breaks, working hours may not exceed 40 a week or 8 a day. Extended hours or work on days off must be paid at a premium rate between 25% and 50%, no lower than the rate set by cabinet order. The rates set by cabinet order are 25% for extended hours and 35% for work on days off. Extended hours beyond 60 in a month are paid at 50% or more. Work between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. gets an extra 25% or more. Wages must be paid in cash, directly and in full, at least once a month on a fixed schedule. Wage claims under the Labor Standards Act, other than retirement allowances, are currently subject to a 3-year limitation period
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 労働基準法 第二十四条、第三十二条、第三十七条、第百十五条、附則第百四十三条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000049> ; 労働基準法第三十七条第一項の時間外及び休日の割増賃金に係る率の最低限度を定める政令. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/406CO0000000005> ; 裁判所「労働審判手続」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.courts.go.jp/saiban/syurui/syurui_minzi/minzi_25_21/index.html> ; 民事訴訟費用等に関する法律 第二条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/346AC0000000040>
- Notes: To recover unpaid overtime, the first step is to take your records to the Labour Standards Inspection Office (労働基準監督署), which is free. Only if the company still does not pay do you need to consider labour tribunal proceedings (労働審判) or a lawsuit. Labour tribunal proceedings in principle have no more than 3 hearings. Cases concluded from 2006 to 2024 took 82.6 days on average, and 65.5% ended within 3 months. If an objection to the tribunal's decision is filed within 2 weeks, the case becomes a lawsuit. Among the litigation costs listed in the Act on Costs of Civil Procedure, lawyers' fees appear only where the court orders a lawyer appointed, so as a rule you pay for a lawyer you hire yourself. The benefit is rated large because unpaid wages often add up to hundreds of thousands of yen. Students' part-time work has a 28-hour weekly limit, and overtime counts toward it; see Section 2, entry 10 (no more than 28 hours a week).

### 2. If you are injured at work or on your commute, apply for workers' accident compensation at the Labour Standards Inspection Office: treatment is free, and from day 4 off work you get 80%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 上班或通勤路上受了伤，去劳动基准监督署申请劳灾：治疗免费，停工第 4 天起领 80% | 636c4ba3 -->
- Cost: Free. Go to a hospital designated for workers' accident compensation, tell the doctor you were injured at work, and use workers' accident compensation (労災) instead of health insurance. Submit the forms to the Labour Standards Inspection Office; if the company will not help, you can submit them yourself.
- In plain words: Workers' accident insurance applies regardless of nationality, including students injured in part-time work. Treatment at a designated hospital is free. If you cannot work because of the injury and are not paid, from day 4 you get 80% of your usual daily pay, and for the first 3 days the employer pays 60%. Claims for treatment and time off must be made within 2 years.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) states that workers' accident compensation insurance applies to foreigners working in Japan as workers regardless of nationality; those with work status are of course covered, and so are students who have an accident in part-time work. The Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Act provides that medical treatment compensation is the treatment itself, including examination, medicine, surgery and hospitalization. MHLW states that at workers' accident hospitals and designated medical institutions and pharmacies, treatment and medicine are free. If you cannot work because of injury or illness from work or commuting and are not paid wages, from day 4 off work you are paid a leave compensation benefit of 60% of the daily benefit base, plus a special leave payment of 20%. The first 3 days are a waiting period, for which, in the case of a work injury, the employer pays 60% of average wages. Claims for treatment, leave and similar benefits expire after 2 years; disability and survivors' benefits after 5 years. Some benefits cannot be received after returning to your country of origin
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 労働者災害補償保険法 第十三条、第十四条、第四十二条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000050> ; 厚生労働省労働基準局 労災補償部補償課「［日本で働く外国人向け］労災保険請求のためのガイドブック ＜第二編＞ 労災保険給付の概要」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/new-info/kobetu/roudou/gyousei/kantoku/dl/161108-22.pdf>
- Notes: If your company tells you to "use health insurance and don't report it as a work accident", do not go along; a work injury belongs under workers' accident compensation. MHLW gives an example: for someone earning 200,000 yen a month, the daily benefit base is about 6,522 yen, and one day off work pays about 5,217 yen. If you plan to return to your country of origin, ask the Labour Standards Inspection Office beforehand which benefits you can still receive after you go.

### 3. An hourly wage below your prefecture's minimum wage is illegal; for fiscal 2026 Tokyo's is 1,280 yen
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 时薪低于所在都道府县的最低工资就是违法，2026 年度东京是 1,280 日元 | d55cce27 -->
- Cost: Free. Compare your hourly wage with your prefecture's minimum wage. If you are paid monthly, divide your monthly pay by your monthly working hours to get an hourly rate and compare that.
- In plain words: Every prefecture in Japan has a minimum wage, and it applies to foreigners too. For fiscal 2026 the national average is 1,177 yen and Tokyo's is 1,280 yen. It took effect on October 3, 2026. Kanagawa is 1,279 yen and Osaka 1,231 yen. Effective dates differ by area, and rates rise around October each year.
- Benefit: MHLW has compiled the fiscal 2026 regional minimum wages recommended by each local minimum wage council: the national weighted average is 1,177 yen, 56 yen higher than the previous year's 1,121 yen. All 47 prefectures rose by 54 to 65 yen, with a high of 1,280 yen and a low of 1,085 yen. Tokyo is 1,280 yen, effective from October 3, 2026; Kanagawa 1,279 yen, from October 1; Aichi 1,195 yen, from October 24; Osaka 1,231 yen. The Labor Standards Act provides that employers may not discriminate in wages, working hours or other working conditions on the ground of a worker's nationality
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 厚生労働省「全ての都道府県で地域別最低賃金の改定額が答申されました」（令和８年度）. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_75950.html> ; 同別紙「令和８年度地域別最低賃金改定状況」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11302000/001745621.pdf> ; 労働基準法 第三条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000049>
- Notes: The table shows the councils' recommended amounts; the official amount in each area follows the decision of the prefectural labour bureau. The benefit is rated medium because a gap of tens to over 100 yen an hour means a monthly gap of several thousand to 10,000 or 20,000 yen. If you are paid below the minimum wage, you can report it to the Labour Standards Inspection Office; see entry 7 (labour helplines in Chinese).

### 4. Get the working conditions in writing before you sign; if the reality differs from what was written, you can quit on the spot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 签约前要一份书面的劳动条件，实际和写的不一样，可以当场辞职 | 5f6589c7 -->
- Cost: Free. Before signing, ask for a notice of working conditions stating pay, working hours and duties. If you cannot understand it, photograph it and get someone to read it for you.
- In plain words: When hiring, a company must tell you the conditions such as pay and working hours, and pay and working hours must be given in writing. If after starting you find the actual conditions differ from what you were told, you can end the contract immediately. If you moved for the job, the company pays your fare back home if you return within 14 days.
- Benefit: The Labor Standards Act provides that when concluding a labour contract, the employer must clearly state working conditions such as wages and working hours to the worker; matters set by MHLW ordinance, including wages and working hours, must be stated by the method the ordinance sets. If the stated conditions differ from the facts, the worker may cancel the labour contract immediately. If a worker who moved to take up the job returns to their former home within 14 days of cancellation, the employer must pay the necessary travel costs
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 労働基準法 第十五条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000049>
- Notes: The benefit is rated medium because you can get out immediately and recover travel costs. If you cannot read the contract language, you can first ask a labour helpline in Chinese; see entry 7 (labour helplines in Chinese). If the job does not match your status of residence, your residence is also affected; see Section 2, entry 10 (get permission for activities outside your status before working part-time).

### 5. If you are dismissed, the company must give at least 30 days' notice or pay 30 days' wages; a dismissal without reasonable grounds is invalid
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 被解雇，公司至少要提前 30 天通知，不然要付 30 天工资；没有合理理由的解雇无效 | 6bea53b3 -->
- Cost: Free. Ask the company to state the reason for dismissal in writing, and do not sign documents you do not understand.
- In plain words: To dismiss you, the company must tell you at least 30 days ahead; without notice, it must pay at least 30 days' average wages. A dismissal without objectively reasonable grounds, which does not make sense by common standards, is invalid in law. On a fixed-term contract, you cannot be dismissed before the term ends without unavoidable reasons. After leaving a job, there are also deadlines for your residence to keep in mind.
- Benefit: The Labor Standards Act provides that an employer dismissing a worker must give at least 30 days' notice. Without 30 days' notice, the employer must pay at least 30 days' average wages. Each day of average wages paid shortens the notice period by 1 day. The Labor Contracts Act provides that a dismissal lacking objectively reasonable grounds and not considered appropriate in light of social norms is invalid as an abuse of rights. On a fixed-term labour contract, a worker cannot be dismissed before the term ends unless there are unavoidable reasons
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 労働基準法 第二十条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000049> ; 労働契約法 第十六条、第十七条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/419AC0000000128>
- Notes: Whether a dismissal is "invalid" is decided by a court; contesting it means labour tribunal proceedings or a lawsuit, and the process costs are in the notes to entry 1 (overtime pay). First call a labour helpline to understand your position; see entry 7 (labour helplines in Chinese). After leaving a job you must report to immigration within 14 days and find matching work within 3 months; see Section 2, entry 3 (reporting after leaving or changing jobs) and Section 2, entry 8 (do not stay idle for more than 3 months after leaving a job or school). The benefit is rated medium because the payment in lieu of notice is usually about a month's wages.

### 6. Neither the company nor the supervising organization may keep a technical intern's passport or residence card
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 技能实习生的护照和在留卡，公司和监理团体都不能替你保管 | 58707f94 -->
- Cost: Free. Keep your passport and residence card yourself. If you are asked to hand them over, you can refuse.
- In plain words: A technical intern training company or supervising organization that keeps an intern's passport or residence card against their will can be sentenced to imprisonment. They also cannot restrict your going out, agree penalties for breach of contract, or make you deposit "security money". If the company breaks the law, you can report it to the Immigration Services Agency and MHLW, and the company cannot treat you badly for reporting.
- Benefit: The Technical Intern Training Act provides that implementing organizations, supervising organizations and their staff may not keep a technical intern's passport or residence card, and may not unduly restrict the intern's freedom in private life, such as going out. Keeping a passport or residence card against the intern's will is punishable by imprisonment of up to 6 months or a fine of up to 300,000 yen. Supervising organizations and others may not agree with the intern or their relatives on penalties or liquidated damages for breaching the training contract, may not have the intern enter into an attached savings contract, and may not manage the intern's savings. If an implementing organization or others violate the Act, the intern may report it to the Commissioner of the Immigration Services Agency and the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare, and may not have their training stopped or be otherwise treated unfavourably for reporting. The Immigration Services Agency states that the technical intern training system will be replaced by the Employment for Skill Development system, and the amended law, apart from some provisions, takes effect on April 1, 2027
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 外国人の技能実習の適正な実施及び技能実習生の保護に関する法律 第四十七条、第四十八条、第四十九条、第百十一条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/428AC0000000089> ; 出入国在留管理庁「育成就労制度の制度概要・関係法令」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/03_00163.html>
- Notes: The benefit is rated medium because it protects your documents and freedom of movement, but the penalty is relatively light. How current interns transition will follow the transitional measures published by the Immigration Services Agency. You also may not lend your residence card to anyone; see Section 5, entry 1 (do not lend, buy or sell a residence card).

### 7. If you are unsure about pay, overtime or dismissal, call MHLW's labour helplines in Chinese
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
<!-- zh: 工资、加班、解雇的事拿不准，打厚生劳动省的中文劳动咨询电话 | 968968e4 -->
- Cost: Numbers starting with 0120 are free; numbers starting with 0570 charge for the call.
- In plain words: MHLW has two phone lines in Chinese. The Working Conditions Hotline, 0120-531-402, is free and open every day. On weekdays it runs from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., and on weekends and holidays from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. The consultation line for foreign workers, 0570-001-702, runs on weekdays from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., with a lunch break.
- Benefit: MHLW states that the "consultation line for foreign workers" takes questions from foreign workers in 13 languages, explaining laws on working conditions and referring callers to relevant bodies. The Chinese line is 0570-001-702, Monday to Friday, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., except 12 noon to 1 p.m.; it costs 9.35 yen per 180 seconds from a landline and 11 yen per 20 seconds from a mobile. The "Working Conditions Hotline" in Chinese is 0120-531-402, open every day, weekdays 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., and Saturdays, Sundays and holidays 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
- Evidence: B
- Sources: 厚生労働省「外国人労働者向け相談ダイヤルの御案内」. <https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/001708139.pdf> ; 厚生労働省「労働条件相談「ほっとライン」(Working Hotline)」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.check-roudou.mhlw.go.jp/lp/hotline/>
- Notes: Why grade B: the numbers and hours are stated officially, but there is no figure for how much time it saves. The phone lines explain laws and refer you to bodies; to get the company to correct things, you still need to go to the Labour Standards Inspection Office. Regional labour bureaus also have consultation counters for foreign workers; see your prefectural labour bureau's page.

### 8. When you leave a job, ask the company for a certificate of employment (退職証明書) stating your period of employment, duties and reason for leaving; if dismissed, you can also ask for the reason for dismissal to be stated
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
<!-- zh: 离职时向公司要一份退職証明書，写明在职期间、工作内容和离职理由，被解雇的还可以要求写上解雇理由 | 372c5710 -->
- Cost: Free. Just ask the company, preferably by email or in writing so there is a record.
- In plain words: When you leave and ask for it, the company must promptly issue a certificate of employment stating your period of employment, duties, position, pay or reason for leaving. If you are dismissed, you can ask for the reason for dismissal to be stated from the day you are given notice. The company may not include items you did not ask for. Refusing to issue it is illegal and can be punished with a fine of up to 300,000 yen.
- Benefit: Article 22 of the Labor Standards Act provides that when a worker, on leaving, requests a certificate on the period of employment, type of work, position in the business, wages or reason for leaving (including the reason for dismissal), the employer must issue it without delay. When a worker, between receiving notice of dismissal and the day of leaving, requests a certificate stating the reason for dismissal, the employer must also issue it without delay. The certificate may not include items the worker did not request; the employer may not, by prior arrangement with third parties and to obstruct the worker's employment, communicate the worker's nationality, creed, social status or union activities, nor put secret marks on the certificate. Article 120 of the Labor Standards Act provides that violations of Article 22, paragraphs 1 to 3 of that Act are punishable by a fine of up to 300,000 yen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 労働基準法 第 22 条、第 120 条. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/322AC0000000049>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. The certificate of employment is not the same as the separation notice (離職票) used to claim unemployment benefits; for the separation notice, see Section 9, entry 3 (take your separation notice to Hello Work). Whether the reason for leaving is the company's or your own affects whether you must wait before unemployment benefits start. If the company refuses, first call a labour helpline in Chinese to find out what to do; see entry 7 of this section (labour helplines in Chinese), then go to the Labour Standards Inspection Office, which is free. The benefit is rated small because it is the document itself.
