# 2. Residence status: keep your visa out of trouble

<!-- zh-intro: 6657ee46 -->

This section covers the reporting deadlines and limits attached to residence status, and the situations in which your status can be revoked. The statute has been checked against the version of the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act (出入国管理及び難民認定法) in force from 2026-10-01, called the Immigration Act below. Content from the Immigration Services Agency's pages is as of 2026-10-03. Immigration rules change quickly, so check the official pages of the Immigration Services Agency of Japan (出入国在留管理庁) again before you act.

In this section, "mid- to long-term residents" (中長期在留者) means foreigners living in Japan with a residence card. People here on a short-term stay (短期滞在) for tourism or family visits, and people whose period of stay is 3 months or less, are not included.

### 1. Carry your residence card when you go out, and show it when the police ask
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 出门随身带在留卡，警察要看就给看 | f9276a2c -->
- Cost: Free. Before you leave home, check that your residence card (在留カード) is in your wallet.
- In plain words: Keep your residence card on you at all times. Not carrying it can get you fined. Refusing to show it when police or immigration officers ask is punished more heavily, and a prison sentence can also lead to deportation. Children under 16 do not have to carry it.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, mid- to long-term residents must carry their residence card at all times. If you carry the residence card, you do not also need to carry your passport. Not carrying the residence card is punishable by a fine (罰金) of up to 200,000 yen. When immigration inspectors, immigration control officers, police officers or other officials ask you to show it while performing their duties, you must show it. Refusing is punishable by imprisonment (拘禁刑) of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen. A mid- to long-term resident sentenced to imprisonment for refusing to show the card falls under the grounds for deportation (退去強制). Foreigners under 16 do not have to carry it
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法（昭和二十六年政令第三百十九号，version in force from 2026-10-01）第二十三条第二項・第三項・第四項・第五項、第七十五条の二第二号、第七十五条の三、第二十四条第四号の四. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: The same article says that an official who asks for your card must show their own identification if you request it. If you are not sure the person is really a police officer, ask them to show their ID first. In Japan a fine (罰金) is a criminal penalty. The benefit is rated large because it avoids criminal liability.

### 2. Within 14 days of moving, register your move-in at the new city office and bring your residence card
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 搬家后 14 天内去新住所的区役所办转入，带上在留卡 | 6e400460 -->
- Cost: Free. Go once to the city, ward, town or village office (市区町村役所) of your new address and hand over your residence card when you file the move-in for your residence record (住民票).
- In plain words: Within 14 days of moving, register at the office for your new address and bring your residence card. That also counts as reporting to immigration, so you do not need a separate trip. Reporting late can get you fined. If you have not reported a new address 90 days after moving out, your status can be revoked. Reporting a false address can also get it revoked.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, a mid- to long-term resident who moves must report the new address to immigration through the mayor of the new municipality within 14 days of moving in, and must hand over the residence card when doing so. Filing a move-in notice (転入届) or change-of-address notice (転居届) under the Basic Resident Registration Act while handing over the residence card counts as reporting to immigration. Failing to report on time is punishable by a fine of up to 200,000 yen. If you have not reported a new address within 90 days of leaving your old one, the Minister of Justice may revoke your status of residence, unless you have a legitimate reason. Reporting a false address can also lead to revocation, and is punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第十九条の九第一項・第三項、第七十一条の五第二号、第二十二条の四第一項第九号・第十号、第七十一条の二第一号. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: The statute says the report to immigration only counts if you hand over the residence card when filing the move-in, so always bring the card to the counter. If you move within the same municipality, you file a change-of-address notice (転居届), and you also need to hand over the card.

### 3. Report to immigration within 14 days of leaving a job, changing jobs or changing schools; you can do it online
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 离职、换工作、转学之后 14 天内向入管申报，网上就能办 | f96832b4 -->
- Cost: Free. Use immigration's online notification system; the first time you need to register an account. You can also go to a counter or report by mail.
- In plain words: If you hold a work visa and leave or change your employer, or you are a student and transfer or drop out, you must tell immigration within 14 days. Not reporting can get you fined. You can report online at any hour, with no extra documents. If you are the spouse of a Japanese national or permanent resident and you divorce or your spouse dies, you must also report within 14 days.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, the following mid- to long-term residents must report to the Commissioner of the Immigration Services Agency within 14 days of the event. People with statuses such as Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, Skilled Labor, Specified Skilled Worker, Nursing Care or Researcher must report when their contract with the employer ends or a new contract is signed, or when the employer changes its name, moves or ceases to exist. People with statuses such as Student, Business Manager, Instructor, Intra-company Transferee or Technical Intern Training must report when they leave or move to another affiliated organization. People staying as the spouse of a Japanese national or permanent resident must report a divorce or the spouse's death. Failing to report on time is punishable by a fine of up to 200,000 yen. A false report is punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen. The Immigration Services Agency's page states that you can report online through the electronic notification system 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, with no supporting documents. You can also go to a regional immigration counter, or mail the report form and a copy of your residence card to the Tokyo Regional Immigration Services Bureau
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第十九条の十六第一号・第二号・第三号、第七十一条の五第三号、第七十一条の二第一号. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 出入国在留管理庁「所属（契約）機関に関する届出」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/nyuukokukanri10_00015.html> ; 出入国在留管理庁「所属（活動）機関に関する届出」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/nyuukokukanri10_00014.html> ; 出入国在留管理庁 電子届出システムポータルサイト. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/online/i-ens_index.html>
- Notes: Leaving a job and starting a new one are two separate events, reported separately. If you find a new job some time after leaving, report once within 14 days of leaving and again within 14 days of starting. If you report by mail, immigration will not confirm receipt, so the agency recommends a mailing method with tracking. There is also a 3-month limit after leaving a job; see entry 8 (do not stay idle for more than 3 months after leaving a job or school).

### 4. When you leave Japan for a trip home, tick "temporary departure" on the re-entry ED card, and come back within 1 year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 回国探亲出境时，在再入国 ED 卡上勾选「一时出国」，1 年内回来 | e369ed89 -->
- Cost: Free. When you leave, tick a box on the re-entry ED card and tell the inspector you are leaving under the special re-entry permit (みなし再入国許可). If you will be abroad for 1 year or more, get a regular re-entry permit from immigration in advance.
- In plain words: Tick "I plan to return" when you leave, and you will not need a new visa if you are back within 1 year. If you leave without ticking it, your status of residence ends on the spot. The 1 year cannot be extended from abroad. If you need longer, get a regular re-entry permit before leaving; it lasts up to 5 years.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, a mid- to long-term resident with a valid passport and residence card who tells the immigration inspector on departure that they will re-enter is treated as having a re-entry permit. It is valid for 1 year from the day of departure. If the period of stay expires within 1 year, the period of stay applies instead. The rules for extending the validity abroad do not apply to it. A regular re-entry permit is valid for up to 5 years. The Immigration Services Agency's page states that, in practice, you tick the box for temporary departure with planned re-entry on the re-entry embarkation record (re-entry ED card), hand it to the inspector, and say you are leaving under the special re-entry permit. The page also states that if you leave without a re-entry permit (including the special one), your status of residence and period of stay end, and you need a new visa to come back to Japan
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十六条の二第一項・第二項・第三項、第二十六条第三項. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 出入国在留管理庁「みなし再入国許可（入管法第26条の2）」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/immigration/procedures/minashisainyukoku_00001.html> ; 出入国在留管理庁「再入国許可（入管法第26条）」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/immigration/procedures/sainyukoku_00002.html>
- Notes: People whose status of residence is in the process of being revoked, and people the Minister of Justice has designated for separate review, cannot use the special re-entry permit and need a regular one. Before you leave, check the expiry date on your residence card. If it expires within 1 year, you must be back before it does.

### 5. Do not commit crimes such as theft, fraud or assault: a prison sentence can get work-visa holders and students deported
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 别犯盗窃、诈骗、伤害这类罪：判了刑，工作签和留学生可能被遣返 | 81c42c45 -->
- Cost: Free.
- In plain words: If you hold a work visa, a student visa or a Dependent visa and are sentenced to imprisonment for theft, fraud, assault or similar crimes, you can be deported even if the sentence is suspended. Whatever your status, the same applies to an actual prison term of more than 1 year. A sentence for a drug crime can also lead to deportation.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, holders of an Appended Table I status who are sentenced to imprisonment for Penal Code offences such as trespass, counterfeiting currency or documents, gambling, homicide, injury, unlawful arrest and confinement, kidnapping, theft and robbery, fraud and extortion, or handling stolen goods fall under the grounds for deportation. Offences under the Act on Prevention and Disposition of Robbery and Theft and the offence of dangerous driving causing death or injury also count. This provision does not require the sentence to be served. Regardless of status of residence, a sentence of life imprisonment or imprisonment for more than 1 year also falls under the grounds for deportation. However, a fully suspended sentence, or a partly suspended sentence where the part actually served is 1 year or less, is excluded from this provision. Being sentenced for violating drug-related laws also falls under the grounds for deportation
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十四条第四号の二・第四号チ・第四号リ. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 刑法 第二編第十二章、第十六章から第十九章、第二十三章、第二十六章、第二十七章、第三十一章、第三十三章、第三十六章、第三十七章、第三十九章（章名）. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/140AC0000000045>
- Notes: The statute lists chapters of the Penal Code. This entry turns the chapter names into common offence names. Embezzlement (横領) is not among them. Whether a particular offence counts is decided by the text of the statute, and if it actually happens to you, have a lawyer check. What happens after an arrest, and how to get a lawyer, will be covered in the section on Japan's legal red lines.

### 6. Apply to renew your period of stay 3 months before it expires; do not leave it to the last few days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 在留期间到期前 3 个月就申请更新，别拖到最后几天 | e930cbfb -->
- Cost: Applying early costs nothing extra. You have to pay the fee anyway. From 2026-10-01 it depends on the period granted, from 10,000 to 75,000 yen; a 1-year grant costs 33,000 yen. Preparing the documents takes a few hours, and you can apply online.
- In plain words: You can apply to renew from 3 months before your stay expires. If you apply before expiry and have no decision yet, you can stay legally while you wait, for up to 2 months after the original expiry date. If you have not applied by the expiry date, you are overstaying and can be prosecuted and deported.
- Benefit: The Immigration Services Agency's page states that people with a period of stay of 6 months or more can apply for renewal from 3 months before it expires, or earlier in special circumstances. Under the Immigration Act, if you apply before your period of stay expires and no decision has been made by the expiry date, you may remain in Japan under your current status. This lasts until the decision is made or until 2 months after the original expiry date, whichever comes first. People with a period of stay of 30 days or less are excluded. Remaining in Japan after expiry without renewal is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 3 million yen, or both, and falls under the grounds for deportation. The agency's page states that from 2026-10-01 the renewal fee depends on the period granted: 10,000 yen for 3 months or less, 18,000 yen for over 3 up to 6 months, 25,000 yen for over 6 months up to 1 year, 33,000 yen for 1 year, 48,000 yen for over 1 up to 3 years, 64,000 yen for over 3 up to 5 years, and 75,000 yen for over 5 years. Applications accepted on or before 2026-09-30 still pay the previous 6,000 yen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国在留管理庁「在留期間更新許可申請」（accessed 2026-10-03）. <https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/16-3.html> ; 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十一条第四項（準用第二十条第六項）、第七十条第一項第五号、第二十四条第四号ロ. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: The fee used to be 6,000 yen regardless of the period granted, and that old figure still circulates online; it is no longer correct. People who meet certain conditions can get a reduced fee of 10,000 yen regardless of the period granted; see the agency's page for the conditions.

### 7. If you lose your residence card, apply to immigration for a replacement within 14 days of finding out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 在留卡丢了，知道之后 14 天内去入管补办 | 8ed433ec -->
- Cost: One trip to the regional immigration office for the replacement.
- In plain words: If your residence card is lost, stolen or burned, apply to immigration for a replacement within 14 days of finding out. If you lose it abroad, the 14 days count from the day you return to Japan. Not applying on time can lead to a prison sentence or a fine. When the card itself is about to expire, you must also replace it on time.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, a mid- to long-term resident who loses the residence card through loss, theft, destruction or similar must apply for a reissue within 14 days of learning of it. If you learn of it while abroad, the period counts from your next entry into Japan. Violations are punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen. If the card's validity ends before the period of stay, you must apply to renew the card between 3 months before its expiry and the expiry date. Violating this carries the same penalty
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第十九条の十二第一項、第十九条の十一第一項、第七十一条の二第二号. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: The card's expiry date and your period of stay are not always the same day. Permanent residents have no limit on their period of stay, but the card itself has an expiry date and must be replaced before then.

### 8. After leaving a job or school, do not stay idle for more than 3 months; find matching work or change your status quickly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 离职或退学后别空着超过 3 个月，尽快找到对口的工作或者改资格 | 73d97b19 -->
- Cost: No direct money. Within 3 months you need to find work that matches your status of residence, or apply to change your status. The time spent job hunting and the stress are the main costs.
- In plain words: With a work or student status, if you go 3 months or more without doing the activity your status is for, your status can be revoked. For spouses of Japanese nationals or permanent residents, the limit is 6 months of not living as a spouse. Legitimate reasons are an exception, and immigration hears your explanation before revoking.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, the Minister of Justice may revoke the status of residence of a holder of an Appended Table I status who stays in Japan without engaging in the activity of that status for 3 months or more in a row, unless there is a legitimate reason. Appended Table I covers statuses defined by activity, such as work, study and Dependent. For Highly Skilled Professional (ii) the limit is 6 months. People staying as the spouse of a Japanese national or permanent resident who do not live as a spouse for 6 months or more in a row can also have their status revoked. Before revocation, an immigration inspector must hear the person, who may attend with or through a representative, make statements and submit evidence. After revocation, a departure preparation period of up to 30 days is usually granted. Staying beyond it is punishable by imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 3 million yen, or both. For the spouse category, the Minister of Justice must consider giving the person a chance to apply for a change of status or for permanent residence
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第二十二条の四第一項第六号・第七号・第二項・第四項・第七項、第二十二条の五、第七十条第一項第三号の三. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: The statute does not list what counts as a "legitimate reason". If you are job hunting after leaving a job, keep records of applications and interviews so you can show them if asked. The new job must match your status; if it does not, change your status first. For the 14-day report after leaving a job, see entry 3 (reporting after leaving or changing jobs).

### 9. Even with permanent residence, your residence card expires: renew the card at immigration within 3 months before expiry, or face up to 1 year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 拿到永住也别忘了在留卡有有效期：到期前 3 个月内去入管更新卡，不更新最重判 1 年 | 6577da7b -->
- Cost: The application itself is free. Within 3 months before the card expires, go to immigration with your passport, residence card and a photo.
- In plain words: Permanent residents do not renew their period of stay, but the residence card itself expires. For adults it is valid until the 10th birthday after issue, and for those under 18 until the 5th birthday. Apply for a new card within 3 months before it expires. Not doing so is illegal, with a maximum penalty of imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen.
- Benefit: Article 19-5 of the Immigration Act provides that a permanent resident's card is valid until the 10th birthday after the date of issue (for a replacement card, after the expiry date of the old card); for permanent residents under 18 at issue, until the 5th birthday; and for others until the end of the period of stay. Article 19-11 of the Immigration Act provides that a mid- to long-term resident whose card is not valid until the end of the period of stay must apply to renew the card between 3 months before its expiry and the expiry date; with unavoidable reasons, they may apply earlier. Article 71-2 of the Immigration Act provides that violating Article 19-11, paragraph 1 is punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 200,000 yen
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第 19 条の 5、第 19 条の 11、第 71 条の 2. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319>
- Notes: As of 2026-10-03. Highly Skilled Professional (ii) holders also count by the 10th birthday. For work visas and other statuses with a period that needs renewal, the card follows the period of stay; see entry 6 of this section (apply to renew 3 months before expiry). The expiry date is printed on the card, so just mark it on your calendar. The benefit is rated medium because it avoids a criminal penalty affecting your residence.

### 10. Get permission for activities outside your status before working part-time, stay within 28 hours a week, and avoid adult entertainment businesses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
<!-- zh: 拿到资格外活动许可再打工，每周不超过 28 小时，不去风俗营业的店 | 02a6b20f -->
- Cost: Applying for permission for activities outside your status (資格外活動許可) takes one round of paperwork. The real cost is lost earnings: you cannot earn income beyond the hour limit.
- In plain words: Students and Dependent visa holders need permission for activities outside their status before working part-time, and can work up to 28 hours a week. During long school holidays set by their school, students can work up to 8 hours a day. Hostess bars, pachinko parlours and mahjong parlours all count as adult entertainment businesses (風俗営業), and you cannot do any work there at all. Violations can lead to a criminal sentence, and serious cases to deportation.
- Benefit: Under the Immigration Act, holders of an Appended Table I status may not engage in paid activities outside their status without permission. The enforcement regulations provide that permission granted with general conditions covers paid activities of up to 28 hours a week. For students during long holidays set by the school's rules, the limit is 8 hours a day. Activities carried out at the premises of adult entertainment businesses, store-type sex-related special businesses and the like are outside the permission. Adult entertainment businesses under the Adult Entertainment Business Act include places equipped to have staff drink with and entertain customers, and places such as mahjong parlours and pachinko parlours where customers play games with an element of gambling. Engaging in activities outside your status without permission is punishable by imprisonment of up to 1 year or a fine of up to 2 million yen, or both. Where it is clearly your main occupation, the penalty is imprisonment of up to 3 years or a fine of up to 3 million yen, or both, and it falls under the grounds for deportation. Being sentenced to imprisonment for the first offence also falls under the grounds for deportation
- Evidence: A
- Sources: 出入国管理及び難民認定法 第十九条第一項・第二項、第七十三条第一号、第七十条第一項第四号、第二十四条第四号イ・ヘ. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/326CO0000000319> ; 出入国管理及び難民認定法施行規則（昭和五十六年法務省令第五十四号，version in force from 2026-10-01）第十九条第五項第一号. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/356M50000010054> ; 風俗営業等の規制及び業務の適正化等に関する法律 第二条第一項第一号・第四号. <https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/law/323AC0000000122>
- Notes: The regulations say "activities carried out at the premises of" adult entertainment businesses, so washing dishes or cleaning there is also not allowed. Work-visa holders who take a paid side job outside their main work are also engaging in activities outside their status, so check first whether it is allowed.

- In plain words: If you are not sure whether or how to report something, call immigration's helpline at 0570-013904; Chinese is available. You do not need to pay an agent. Immigration counters in Tokyo, Yokohama, Nagoya, Osaka and other cities can also answer in Chinese. The first working day after a holiday, and 11 a.m. to noon, are the hardest times to get through.
